Are your Air Handling Unit (AHU) operating costs skyrocketing while air quality downstream continues to deteriorate? Facilities management teams often notice rapid airflow reduction, high energy bills, and premature system cleanings. Selecting the wrong combination of air filters for an AHU system leads to unbalanced static pressure, frequent equipment downtime, and inadequate indoor air quality (IAQ) compliance.
Air filtration in an AHU works on the principle of gradient particle separation. Air contains particles ranging from visible dust (>10 μm) to microscopic airborne viruses (<0.3 μm). Attempting to catch all contaminants with a single, high-efficiency filter causes immediate surface loading, triggering an exponential rise in fan resistance. According to fan affinity laws, every additional 100 Pa of differential pressure forces the supply fan to consume significantly more kilowatt-hours to maintain designed CFM.
TABLE:
  Filter Class | Typical Efficiency | Primary Role | Common Form Factor
  Primary (G1–G4 / Coarse) | Traps particles >10 μm (pollen, lint, hair) | Protects heating/cooling coils and secondary filters | Synthetic panel, pleated filter, air filter roll
  Secondary (M5–F9 / ePM10, ePM1) | Traps 1–10 μm fine dust, smoke, mold spores | Protects ductwork, indoor occupants, and HEPA filters | Pocket / bag filter, rigid cell filter
  Final / HEPA (H10–H14) | Traps down to 0.3 μm at 99.97%+ efficiency | Delivers ultra-clean air for sensitive environments | Mini-pleat, deep-pleat HEPA box filter

To build an optimal multi-stage configuration, match your facility’s requirements:
- Standard Commercial Buildings (Offices, Malls): 2-Stage System → G4 Panel Filter (Pre-filter) + F7 Pocket Filter (Secondary).
- Industrial & Food Processing: 2 or 3-Stage System → G4 Pleated Filter + F8 Bag Filter + Optional Activated Carbon Stage (for odor/VOCs).
- Pharmaceutical & Semiconductor Cleanrooms: 3-Stage System → G4 Panel + F9 Rigid Cell Filter + H13/H14 HEPA Filter.
- Skipping the Pre-Filter: Installing an F7 or HEPA filter without a G4 pre-filter ruins the expensive fine filter within weeks.
- Ignoring Total Cost of Ownership (TCO): Buying cheap filters with high initial resistance costs 3x to 5x more in electricity over the filter’s lifespan than investing in low-resistance media.
- Changing Filters Only on a Fixed Calendar: Replacing filters on a rigid 6-month schedule instead of monitoring actual pressure differential (ΔP) leads to either wasted lifespan or severe fan overload.

### How do I know when my AHU filters need replacement?
Always monitor the differential pressure gauge (e.g., Magnehelic). Replace primary filters when initial pressure doubles, or when secondary filters reach their recommended final resistance (typically 250 Pa for bag filters).
