## Quality Control Process
We implement a comprehensive quality control process to ensure our filter products meet industry standards and customer expectations. Our inspection covers key areas, including material properties, performance, safety, and packaging.
### Environmental & Physical Inspection
- Cleanroom Production – High-efficiency filters are manufactured in cleanroom to prevent contamination.
- Thickness & Weight Check – Ensuring the filter media meets required thickness and weight specifications.
- Size Precision Inspection – Verifying product dimensions for accurate installation and performance.
- Uniformity Assessment – Checking fiber distribution and media consistency to maintain filtration stability.
### Performance & Safety Testing
- Airflow Resistance Test – Measuring resistance levels to ensure optimal airflow and efficiency.
- Dust Holding Capacity Test – Simulating dust load conditions to assess filter longevity and effectiveness.
- Fire & Moisture Resistance Test – Ensuring compliance with fire safety and humidity resistance standards.
### Secure Packaging & Delivery
- Protective Sealing – Applying secure wrapping to keep filters free from dust and contamination.
- Shock-Proof Packaging – Using wooden crates or reinforced packaging to prevent damage during transit.
