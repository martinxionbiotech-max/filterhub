## The Hidden Dangers in Paint Workshops
### Pollution Sources Explained
- Toxic Gas Bomb​: Every liter of paint releases fumes equal to 12 soda cans (1.2kg VOCs), causing dizziness and equipment corrosion.
- ​Sticky Dust Invasion​: 30% of spray particles (smaller than a human hair*) stick to robots, reducing painting precision by up to 40%.
- Silent Killer​: Fluoride gases from metal pre-treatment silently eat away at sensor circuits, triggering 23% more machine failures.
### Reality Check
- Water Filters = Colander​: Let through 1,500+ particles per cm³ (like 35% of dust escaping a vacuum cleaner).
- Carbon Traps = Clogged Sponge​: Carbon filters lose 72% effectiveness during night shifts – workers wake up to 3x higher toxin levels.
- ​Energy Drain​: Running dust collectors costs $18,000/year for a mid-sized factory – same as powering 150 homes daily.
