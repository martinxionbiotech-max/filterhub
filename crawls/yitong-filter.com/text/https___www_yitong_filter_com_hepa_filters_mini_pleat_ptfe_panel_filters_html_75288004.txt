# Mini Pleat PTFE Panel HEPA Filters
PTFE membrane as young filter media in China market. It is used in HEPA filters and ULPA air filters with stable performance and lower energy consumption. PTFE HEPA filters and PTFE ULPA filters are ideal for low noise area, corrosive gas area (especially for hydrofluoric acid) and high clean level area. Really suitable for hospitals, sanatoriums, schools and kindergarten, microelectronics industry, sterile room, laboratory etc.
The Mini Pleat PTFE Panel HEPA Filters are designed to provide superior air filtration in demanding environments. Featuring advanced PTFE (Polytetrafluoroethylene) media, these filters offer exceptional filtration efficiency, capturing up to 99.97% of airborne particles as small as 0.3 microns. The mini-pleat design maximizes surface area while maintaining a compact size, ensuring high airflow rates with minimal pressure drop.
These filters are highly resistant to chemicals, moisture, and abrasion, making them ideal for use in cleanrooms, laboratories, hospitals, and other critical applications where maintaining air purity is essential. The robust construction ensures long service life and reliable performance, even in challenging conditions. Easy to install and maintain, Mini Pleat PTFE Panel HEPA Filters are the perfect choice for facilities that require top-tier air quality control.
Detailed information:
Type: Mini pleat, Compact
Frame material: SUS304, Wood, Anodised extruded aluminum
Filter media: PTFE (Polytetrafluoroethylene) membrane
