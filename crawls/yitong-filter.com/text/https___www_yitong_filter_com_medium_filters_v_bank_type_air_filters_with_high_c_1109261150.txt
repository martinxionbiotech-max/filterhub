# V-Bank Type Air Filters with High Capacity
V-bank type air filter is mainly used for the terminal filtration of air conditioning unit and the efficient preprotection. It has advantages of high efficiency, large air volume, convenient installation, low equipment operation cost.
Frame color: Black, blue, silver
Type: V-shaped, Box-shaped.
1. Mini-pleat compact filter with high efficiency.
2. Low resistance, large air volume.
3. Low operation cost.
4. Small in size and suitable for compact central air-conditioning systems.
