# Bag Type or Pocket Type Filter
Yitong medium efficiency air filter is made of non-woven fabrics and galvanized plate, It can stop particles at the size of 1-5 microns. The thermally bonded tech is used to improve leakproof ness and reduce the possibility of breakage. It has not only the advantages of high throughput, low resistance, and high dirt holding capacity but also can be reusable because it is washable which gives it longer service life.
Type: Panel, Pleated and Pocket.
Filter frame: Paper, ABS, Galvanized sheet, extruded aluminium, stainless steel
Media: Chemical fiber, fibreglass, synthetic, Polypropylene fiber
Filter class: F5-F9
