# Replacement HEPA Air Filter
This HEPA cartridge air filter designed as main filter of commercial air purifier, industrial vacuum cleaner. PTFE membrane as filter media with stable performance and less noise. Micro glass fibre paper as filter media with stable performance and longer service life.
Customerized service preferred.
Detailed information:
Cap material: ABS (Plastic)
Outer / inner protective net: powder coated galvanized mesh
Filter structure: Nylon net (coarse filter) + PP support layer + Multiple pleated layers of glass fibre
Note: PTFE membrane, carbon fibre, filter pad also available.
Benefits of production:
1. Customerized servie preferred
2. SGS test report -- EN 1822
Benefits of our factory:
1. Cleanroom workshop.
2. 100% test in our laboratory.
