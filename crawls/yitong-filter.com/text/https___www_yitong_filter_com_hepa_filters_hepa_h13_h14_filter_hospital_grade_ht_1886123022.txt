# V-Bank Filter - Hospital Grade
This type HEPA H13/H14 filter designed for hospital, sanatoriums, library, laboratory etc.
The most advantage is quietly when working with stable H14 performance.
Our filter media has passed the ATCC VR-740 coronavirus inactivation test of Micronchem Laboratory in United States. This filter media can kill 99.994% of the coronavirus within 5 minutes. It can effectively prevent bacteria, viruses and other microorganisms from breeding on the surface of the purification materials to cause secondary pollution.
HEPA H13 H14 filters are high-efficiency particulate air filters designed to capture and retain extremely fine particles, providing superior air quality in a variety of critical environments. These filters can remove up to 99.95% (H13) and 99.995% (H14) of airborne particles that are as small as 0.3 microns, including dust, pollen, mold spores, bacteria, and even some viruses.
Constructed using high-quality materials, HEPA H13 and H14 filters feature a dense network of fibers arranged in a pleated design, which maximizes the filtration surface area while maintaining a compact footprint. This design ensures high airflow rates and low resistance, making them ideal for applications in cleanrooms, pharmaceutical production, healthcare facilities, and other settings where maintaining ultra-clean air is crucial.
These filters are engineered to meet the strictest air quality standards, providing reliable performance and long service life. Easy to install and maintain, HEPA H13 and H14 filters are the preferred choice for environments that demand the highest levels of air purity and safety.
