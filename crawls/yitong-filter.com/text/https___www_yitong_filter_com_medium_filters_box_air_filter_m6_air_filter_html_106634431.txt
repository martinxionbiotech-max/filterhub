# Box Air Filter M6 Minipleat Filter
This M6 Box Air Filter, with its durable galvanized steel frame, offers reliable and efficient air filtration for various industrial and commercial applications.
The M6 Box Air Filter is designed for efficient air filtration in industrial and commercial HVAC systems. With a medium-efficiency rating (M6), it effectively removes airborne particles, improving indoor air quality while ensuring system longevity. Its robust galvanized steel frame ensures durability and resistance to harsh environmental conditions.
Key Specifications:
- Filter Rating: M6 (EN 779:2012)
- Dimensions: 495 mm x 495 mm x 292 mm
- Frame Material: Galvanized Steel (Corrosion-resistant)
- Filtration Efficiency: 60–80% for 0.4-micron particles
1. Superior Filtration Efficiency:
- Captures dust, pollen, mold spores, and other airborne contaminants, providing a cleaner environment.
- Ideal as a pre-filter for HEPA systems or as a standalone filter for moderate air quality requirements.
2. Robust Galvanized Steel Frame:
- Offers excellent structural stability and resistance to corrosion.
- Suitable for challenging environments such as industrial settings or high-humidity areas.
3. Optimized Airflow Performance:
- Designed to maintain low pressure drops, ensuring efficient airflow and reduced energy consumption.
- Enhances overall HVAC system performance and extends filter life.
4. Versatile Applications:
- Perfect for use in air handling units (AHUs), ventilation systems, clean rooms, and commercial buildings.
- Provides a reliable solution for medium-efficiency filtration needs.
- Industrial HVAC Systems: Ensures clean air in factories and production areas.
- Commercial Buildings: Enhances indoor air quality for offices, shopping malls, and hotels.
- Clean Rooms: Acts as a pre-filter for sensitive environments requiring advanced filtration stages.
- Air Handling Units (AHUs): Integral to maintaining consistent and clean airflow.
- Easy Replacement: The sturdy frame allows for simple installation and removal.
- Extended Lifespan: Routine maintenance ensures long-term performance, reducing operational costs.
This M6 Box Air Filter, with its durable galvanized steel frame, offers reliable and efficient air filtration for various industrial and commercial applications. Its combination of performance, durability, and cost-effectiveness makes it an ideal choice for maintaining optimal air quality.
