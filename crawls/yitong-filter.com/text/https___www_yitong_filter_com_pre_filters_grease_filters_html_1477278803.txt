# Grease Filters
Grease filters are essential components in ventilation and exhaust systems, primarily used in kitchens to capture airborne grease, oils, and particulates. They help prevent grease from accumulating in ducts and exhaust systems, reducing fire hazards and maintaining cleaner air.
Application:
It is widely used in Kitchen for capturing grease from exhausted air. The filter media is stainless steel corrugated mesh, or aluminum corrugated panel. Aluminum corrugated panel is better if working temperature less than 200 degree celsius (392F).
Commercial Kitchens: Essential in restaurant exhaust hoods.
Residential Kitchens: Used in range hoods to keep air clean.
Industrial Environments: Deployed in systems handling oily fumes.
Technical specification:
Frame: aluminum, stainless steel
Filter class: coarse
Filter media: aluminum panel, stainless steel mesh
Lower pressure drop
Protective mesh on both sides
Washable
