# Panel Type Air Filter
Medium Efficiency Panel Air Filter is an economical and effective solution for air filtration. It is composed of a series of panels and frames, which are made of high-quality synthetic fibers. It can effectively filter out dust, pollen, smoke, and other airborne contaminants, while allowing enough air to flow through. It is suitable for use in industrial, commercial and residential settings, and is easy to install and maintain. It also offers a high dust-holding capacity, which ensures that the air is free of contaminants.
Type: Panel, Pleated and Pocket.
Filter frame: Paper, ABS, Galvanized sheet, extruded aluminium, stainless steel
Media: Chemical fiber, fibreglass, synthetic, Polypropylene fiber
Filter class: F5-F9
Initial pressure drop: 50-80Pa
