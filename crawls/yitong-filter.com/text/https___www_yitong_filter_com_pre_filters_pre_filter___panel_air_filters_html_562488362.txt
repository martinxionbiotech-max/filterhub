# Pre-Filter - Panel Air Filters
Panel type air filter is suitable for all kinds of clean ventilation systems’ prefiltration, general ventilation requirements of the central air conditioning heat exchange units’ protection filtration, all kinds of industrial clean workshop or civil constructions’ return air filtration.
Type: Panel, Pleated and Pocket.
Filter frame: Paper, ABS, Galvanized sheet, extruded aluminium, stainless steel
Media: Chemical fiber, stainless steel mesh, nylon mesh
Filter class: G1-G4
Clean air filters pressure drop: 30-50Pa
Standard sizes: Standard depth: 10/21/46/95mm
Customized products prefered.
1. The external border of the product is solid and removable, so it's easy to switch out the filter component.
2. The filter material is constructed of high-grade polyester artificial fiber, making it capable of being washed multiple times and ensuring it lasts longer.
3. It has a high rate of dust collection with a large volume of dust storage.
