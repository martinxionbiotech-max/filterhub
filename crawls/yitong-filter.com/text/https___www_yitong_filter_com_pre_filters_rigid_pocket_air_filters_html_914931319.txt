# Rigid Pocket Air Filters
This type pocket filter is designed as first stage air filtration, also widely used in industrial and commercial area.
The most advantage is high capacity with stable performance.
Our filter media is synthetic fiber with electret. Its initial pressure drop is only ~50% of traditional glass fibre, also good filtering performance.
Detailed information:
Type: Pocket (rigid)
Frame material: Plastic, 25mm
Filter media: Synthetic fibre
Filter class: G3/G4/M5/M6/F7
Initial resistance: ~50% of glass fibre
Standard size: 594 x 594mm, 490 x 592mm
Standard depth: 381/610mm
Benefits of production:
1. Low pressure drop
2. Large capacity
3. V-shaped bag
Benefits of our factory:
1. Clean workshop
2. Complete set testing devices
3. Buyer can monitor working schedule in real time
