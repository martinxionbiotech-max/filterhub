The Carbon V bank filter removes odors as well as dust particles from the ventilation system. It is a multi-functional design, the filter material is formed by compounding a PTFE melt-blown layer and an interlayer in which active carbon particles are arranged. Compared to a normal V bank filter, it also has a plastic frame and standard dimensions of 595 x 595 x 292 mm and 595 x 287 x 292 mm.
Depending on the efficiency of the melt-blown layer, the efficiency of the carbon V bank filter can be arranged from F7, F8, H10 to H13, etc.
The melt-blown layer is a kind of material with good toughness compared with glass fiber, so it can effectively prevent the filter material from being damaged by misoperation during installation. This brings great convenience to the installation.
- Filter media: PTFE with carbon powder combined or carbon particle
- Frame: ABS plastic
- Design: V bank – 2V – 3V – 4V – 5V
- Function: remove dust and odor for ventilation
- Size: for both standard and customized
- Header: 25mm thickness
- Temperature: 80°C
- Humidity: 80%
- Full size: 595*595*292mm
- 80% size: 495*595*292mm
- Half size: 595*287*292mm
