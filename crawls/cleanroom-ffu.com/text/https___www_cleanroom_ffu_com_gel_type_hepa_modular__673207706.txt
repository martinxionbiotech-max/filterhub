Gel-type HEPA Modular is the terminal air supply device of the cleanroom. Its built-in high-efficiency HEPA filter with gel-type sealing design, which can effectively improve the sealing performance of the filter and the box, and improve the air purification capacity. It is particularly important in some special industries such as medical and pharmaceutical.
This kind of equipment can meet the uniformity of air supply. After it is connected to the ventilation duct, the HEPA filter inside it can effectively filter the delivered airflow and evenly diffuse the purified airflow to the coverage area of the room through the diffuser plate, thus ensuring the cleanliness of the room.
This Gel-type HEPA Modular includes a plenum chamber, a diffuser plate, a HEPA filter, a regulating valve, a DOP test port, and an collar with the air duct. It can be connected with the pipe in the way of top connection or side connection. The pipe interface can be designed as a flange and provided with round and square interfaces. The size of the interface can be customized.
