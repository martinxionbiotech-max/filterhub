##### We provide air filter replacement services to pharmaceutical customers
Recently, one of our pharmaceutical customers experienced a significant increase in ventilation system resistance because they didn`t replace the ventilation filters in time. As a result, the air flow is reduced and the air change rate is reduced, which not only affects the air circulation efficiency of the their cleanroom, but also affects the air quality. It also increases power consumption, and high resistance also leads to an increase in energy consumption, causing an unnecessary burden on the operating costs of enterprises.
In order to solve this problem, the customer contact us to provide professional air filter replacement services.
Our team quickly responded to the customer’s needs by conducting a complete inspection of the existing ventilation system and replacing all expired air filters. We have managed to reduce the resistance of the ventilation system by 30% by using our new air filters. This improvement not only improves the efficiency of air circulation in their cleanrooms, but also significantly reduces power consumption, which is a significant amount of money for long-term operating costs.
In order to make customers more worry-free, we have proposed a value-added service-signing an agreement to automatically replace air filters. According to this agreement, we will regularly check the status of the customer’s filters and automatically replace them when necessary, ensuring that the ventilation system is always in the best condition. This service not only reduces the administrative burden on customers, but also further reduces potential operational risks.
We look forward to working with more pharmaceutical companies to help them achieve the dual optimization of production efficiency and cost control.
