## Assemble tips
Firstly, assemble the aluminum tube with connection parts, you may need to check the item list we offered to you. and find the sizes that you need for each step.
(We will cut the aluminum tube into the correct sizes according to the design )
the aluminum tube assembly is the most important step for the whole project.
Second, put FFU on the ceiling, you need to confirm the ceiling tube assembly is accurate to ensure FFU is installed successfully.
Fix the Anti-Static Vinyl Strip on the side wall and cover the rest of the ceiling with cleanroom lights.
Finally, power the connection and fix the control box.
Some accessories, such as feet or universal wheels, need to be installed on the corresponding aluminum square in advance.
If the softwall cleanroom is designed fixed in a certain place, it is recommended to use feet. If it is a small design and needs to be moved when used it, universal wheels are a better choice.
It can also provide other accessories for more customized services.
#### If the softwall cleanroom reliable for purification?
Yes, it is 100% reliable. Maybe you will think that the design is very simple and even the space is not closed. you are worried that contamination will go into the cleanroom. but that will definitely not happen.
Because the FFU fan filter unit blows purified airflow into the inside of the softwall cleanroom. the inside pressure is higher than the outside , only airflow goes out and never goes inside.
You can install a pressure gauge inside the booth, making sure the side pressure is higher than outside.
the FFU fan filter unit creates a 100% purified laminar airflow , the airflow moves from up to down and goes out through the Anti-Static Vinyl Strip. the moving laminar airflow makes sure the working space is purified and away from any contamination.
By using the softwall cleanroom, it can achieve a very good grade of cleanliness, which can be applied to medical, precision machinery, mushrooms, mobile phone laminating, LCD, LED, food and so on.
The available MAX cleanliness level is: ISO5
