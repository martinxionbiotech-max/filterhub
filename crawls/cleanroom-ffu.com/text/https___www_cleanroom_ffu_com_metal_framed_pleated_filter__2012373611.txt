Home » HVAC filter » Metal framed pleated filter
the metal framed pleated filter mainly removes some large-size dust for commercial and industry HVAC systems and air handling units. the metal frame provides a strong construction that makes it extremely durable and heavy-duty.
the filter media is a pleated green-white synthetic fiber with double sides metal grille combined, this dual sides mesh can make the filter stand well in some high-speed airflow blowing situations.
Metal frames can be galvanized steel, extruded aluminum and stainless steel. The metallic frame can let pleated filters anti-rust which means longer service life. Also, this feature contributes to the advantage of being washable, normally this pleated filter can wash 2-3 times with clean water.
The efficiency of these filters is G4 and M5 according to EN: 779 or MERV 6, 8.

## FAQ
the purpose of the pleated filter is to increase the air filter media area, thus evenly separating airflow in a larger filter media surface, letting the pressure drop facing the media decline. As the resistance is lower, the airflow rate of the whole pleated filter is increased. As a professional manufacturer of air filters, we recommend you buy pleated filters with more quantity of filter media plates, you can ask your supplier how many pleats of the filter media. anyway, more pleats mean more airflow rate, less resistance, and even longer service life.
the pleated air filters are mainly used for some general ventilation systems, such as furnaces, air conditioning, commercial and industrial HVAC, and air handling units. those applications require a high airflow rate and heavy-duty dust holding. Because the pleated air filters should be replaced frequently, normally 3 months. So, they are mainly made of cardboard frames , this material is environmentally friendly, and low cost. According to the application, the pleated air filters have different efficiency and airflow designs, the efficiency is G3, G4, and M5 ( Merv 6, 7, 8, 9, etc ) and the pleat quantity varies from normal and heavy-duty designs.
the pleated air filter should be replaced every 3 months, of course, if the surrounding air environment is quite clean, the filters may last longer. If your ventilation system has a pressure drop detection system, you can replace the filter only when pressure reaches a point. Also, you can just simply check your filter to see if the filter media has become dark, if it is become dark it means enough dust and pollution collected. You can set a tip alarm in your phone every 3 months so it can remind you to replace the filter in time. Also, some air filter or ventilation cleaning companies offer filter replacement services, they can help you clean your ventilation duct and replace the filters.
Now, some companies are offering washable pleated filters, but we don`t recommend using it. that air filters adopt the technology of electrostatic adsorption, which will spend electricity and function not so well compared with the normal pleated filters. For some traditional pleated filters, the frame is made of cardboard which means washing with water is not available, and the filter media will be destroyed when washed. Additionally, considering the cost of a filter is only 2-3 USD, if you spend half a day to wash it that is not worth doing. Our metal frame pleated air filter can be washed with water 2-3 times.
Each air filter has its specific advantages. Glassfiber filter is used to collect paint particles. Its fluffy structure allows a lot of paint particles to enter its interior without sticking to each other and causing easy blockage. However, its performance is not as good as other types of filtering ordinary dust. Flat filter usually uses fluffy chemical fibers to be laid flat on the filter frame in a non-folding manner. Its thickness can usually be 5, 10, 15, 25, 45mm. The thickness of 5 and 10 allows it to be used as a filter in some narrow spaces, which is something that pleated filters cannot do. At a thickness of 15, 25, and 45, its filter material will be very thick and have a very good ability to accommodate dust, but because it adopts a non-pleated design, the resistance of the filter will be higher. Foam filter is generally designed to be 3-10mm thick, and often uses a 30PPI porous structure design. Its dust collection ability is the weakest among these filters, but its resistance is very low. The most common application is PC Fan. Its characteristics are that it can be made very small and very thin, suitable for primary filtration in some equipment. Pleated filter requires a design thickness of at least 20mm, and a length and width of at least 10cm. It can provide a variety of efficiencies and reduce filter resistance. The Heavy-duty type can improve dust collection capacity and is the most comprehensive design in terms of overall performance.
the metal frame design can make the pleated filters stand longer in some heavy-duty, high-moisture ventilation systems, metal frame can fix the filter media makes stronger than cardboard, and it is anti-acid, anti-alkali, and anti-moisture. It is a very common design in some coastal and humid countries and regions.
