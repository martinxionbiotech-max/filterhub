A Gel HEPA filter is a specific type of High-Efficiency Particulate Air (HEPA) filter that is designed for use in cleanrooms and pharmaceutical medical laboratories. These environments require a high level of air purity to prevent contamination of products or samples.
High Efficiency : Gel HEPA filters are designed to remove at least 99.97% of airborne particles that are 0.3 micrometers in size. This efficiency rate is crucial in cleanroom and laboratory settings where even microscopic particles can contaminate sensitive processes or products.

Gel HEPA filters are a good choice for air filtration especially for pharma and medical and biotechnology laboratories. It provides a quite good performance to remove particles, bacteria, viruses, and other contaminants from the air.
Gel HEPA filters have a high-efficiency rating that removes up to 99.97% of particles as small as 0.3 microns. This makes them effective at removing airborne contaminants such as PM2.5, bacteria, viruses, and dust.
It brings a low-pressure drop cause made of mini pleat design and the use of high-performance glass fiber filter media, it can be installed in smaller spaces without causing excessive airflow resistance. This is important for applications where space is limited, such as hospital rooms or cleanrooms.
Cause the mini pleat design brings more airflow area, that make it has a better dust-holding ability and a longer service life, typically lasting up to 5 years or more depending on usage and maintenance. This makes them cost-effective compared to other types of air filtration systems.
For laboratories
the Gel HEPA filter for laboratory applications can remove bacteria, fungi, and viruses. there are some reasons why laboratories need HEPA filters. Firstly, to improve the success rate of experiments, fungi and bacteria can affect some biological culture experiments. For culturing a certain type of bacteria alone, fungi. The human phylum does not want additional other microorganisms to affect the final colony formation. HEPA filter installed in a fume hood and bio-safety cabinet can generate a laminar flow from up to down or inside to outside. the purified airflow can prevent any party contamination come in.
At the same time, for some dangerous biological experiments, infectious microorganisms and bacteria cannot be allowed to be directly discharged into the outside world, these microorganisms, dust, etc. need to be collected by HEPA filter. This recycling function also protects the health of experimental staff and reduces the possibility of infection.
the gel-type HEPA filter means the gasket of the HEPA filter is changed, from neoprene or urethane into a semi-solid material ( GEL ). The GEL material can create a much better airtight when it is sealed with a knife edge side on the air filter holding housing.
It depends on the operating conditions of the experimental equipment. Usually, there are pressure detection devices on the Fume hood & Bio-safety Cabinet. When the HEPA filter reaches the final resistance , these devices will prompt that the filter needs to be replaced. You can remove these filters, and you can know the model and specification of the filter by looking at the label & nameplate. When replacing the laboratory HEPA filter, you need to use a plastic bag to seal the old one to prevent the bacteria, viruses and other microorganisms it collects from escaping to the outside.
