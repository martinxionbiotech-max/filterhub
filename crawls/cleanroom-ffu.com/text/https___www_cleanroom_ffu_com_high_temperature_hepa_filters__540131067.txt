## High temperature HEPA filter
High temperature HEPA filter is an air filter that can withstand temperatures up to 250°C . It uses stainless steel as the frame and glass fiber filter paper as the filter material. Widely used in the spraying industry, oven, chemical, ultrasonic industry and other high temperature purification sites.
Red silicon gasket & metal mesh
Organic red silicon gasket and silicon sealant help achieve perfect air-tight in high temperature conditions.
The metal mesh placed on both inlet and outlet side protects filter media from damage.
Stainless steel frame and aluminum spacer
The stainless steel 201 or 304 frame can withstand a high temperature, also it is quite strong to make sure the HEPA filter is robust and stable.
Glassfiber filter media are separated by a corrugated aluminum spacer to form tunnels that airflow passes through.

## Product Design Features
#### Stainless Steel Frame
The frame of our high-temperature HEPA filter is constructed from high-quality stainless steel, a material carefully selected to withstand the harsh conditions of high-temperature industrial environments. Stainless steel boasts excellent thermal stability, maintaining its structural integrity even when exposed to continuous high temperatures (up to 300°C for standard models, with customized versions available for higher temperature needs).
#### Metal Mesh: Reinforced Protection
A durable metal mesh is integrated into the filter design, serving dual critical functions: reinforcement and airflow optimization. Positioned as a protective layer on both the inlet and outlet sides of the filter, the metal mesh prevents physical damage to the internal filter media caused by sudden airflow fluctuations, foreign objects, or accidental impacts during installation and maintenance.
##### High-Temperature Resistant Silicone
Sealing is a key factor in HEPA filter performance, and our design incorporates high-temperature resistant silicone as the core sealing material (with silicone-free alternatives available for silicon-sensitive industries). This specialized silicone is formulated to resist thermal aging, maintaining excellent elasticity and sealing performance even after long-term exposure to high temperatures.
#### Aluminum Separator
Aluminum separators are strategically placed between the pleated filter media to maintain the structure of the filter pack and optimize filtration performance. Aluminum is chosen for its lightweight yet robust properties, as well as its excellent thermal conductivity. The thermal conductivity helps dissipate localized heat evenly across the filter, preventing hot spots that could degrade the filter media over time. The separator’s unique corrugated design creates consistent spacing between pleats, ensuring that each pleat is fully utilized for filtration and avoiding clogging caused by pleat collapse.
#### Glass Fiber Filter Media
Our high-temperature HEPA filter is made of high-performance glass fiber filter media, the key to achieving the HEPA-standard 99.97% filtration efficiency for 0.3-micron particles. The glass fiber is manufactured through a special process that creates a dense, three-dimensional network of microfibers, capturing even the smallest airborne particles, microorganisms, and aerosols. What sets this media apart is its exceptional high-temperature resistance—capable of withstanding continuous temperatures up to 260°C (with high-purity glass fiber options for 300°C+ applications).es.
## Application
#### Food & Pharmaceutical Industries: for Ultra-clean Drying
Food and pharmaceutical industries have extremely high requirements for the cleanliness of the production environment, especially in the key link of material drying.
It is necessary to ensure the drying effect while strictly preventing impurities and microorganisms in the air from contaminating the products; otherwise, the product quality cannot be guaranteed.
Processes such as baked food production, dehydrated vegetable processing in the food industry, and bulk drug drying, pharmaceutical excipient drying in the pharmaceutical industry all require ultra-clean ovens.
#### Chemical & Formaldehyde Factories
In the formaldehyde production process, high-temperature filters must be used to purify the air required for the reaction; otherwise, impurities will affect the reaction efficiency.
However, there is a key issue: the silicone sealant commonly used in ordinary high-temperature filters will react with the catalyst required for production in the formaldehyde production environment, directly causing the catalyst to fail, which will result in huge losses for enterprises.
We adopt a special sealing material that is resistant to chemical corrosion and will not react with the catalyst.
#### Semiconductor and Other Special Industries
There are also industries that are extremely sensitive to “silicon”, such as semiconductors and electronic components. Silicon contamination is absolutely not allowed during the production process; otherwise, it will directly affect the performance of chips and components, and even lead to product scrapping.
We have launched a series of high-temperature HEPA filters with silicone-free design.
From filter material selection, sealing materials to production processes, the introduction of silicon elements is strictly controlled throughout the process.

##### Cleanliness level & application
This is similar to the normal temp HEPA Filter, which achieves an efficiency rating of H13 99.97% @ 0.3micron and H14 99.995% @ 0.3 microns. the cleanliness level makes sure those high temperature filters can be used for pharma , chemical, laboratories, oven, high temp painting spray and so on.
Different efficiencies can be achieved by using special fiberglass materials. Each High-temperature HEPA filter is tested by smoke to ensure that the filter is not leaking. Packed in plastic bags to prevent long-term storage from being oxidized. A single filter is packed in a carton and then secured with wooden boxes to ensure safety during transport.
##### Installation and Usage
Do not use any sharp objects to touch the fragile glass filter. Do not touch the glass filter material with your hand while carrying the filter.
During usage, do not raise the temperature above 250°C in a short time directly. The best way to increase the temperature is to add 50°C per hour. Finally stabilized to between 200°C and 250°C. In order to ensure the service life, it is necessary to add a primary filter, glass fiber primary air filter can be used to collect large particles of dust to enlarge high temperature HEPA filter service life.
