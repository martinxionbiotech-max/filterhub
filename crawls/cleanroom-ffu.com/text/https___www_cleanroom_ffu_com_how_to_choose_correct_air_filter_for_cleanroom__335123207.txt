##### How to choose correct air filter for cleanroom
How to choose the right air filter for a clean room?
Choosing the right air filter is crucial to maintaining the cleanliness and stability of the clean room, which not only affects product quality and production efficiency but also energy consumption and maintenance costs. The following will analyze in detail how to choose the right air filter for a clean room:
Select according to the clean room grade Determine the purification standard: The efficiency of the final level of air filters should be determined according to the purification standard required in the clean room. For example, general purification can use a primary filter, medium purification requires a combination of primary and medium filters, and ultra-clean purification requires primary, medium and high efficiency three-level filtration.
Matching filter efficiency: It is also very important to reasonably match the efficiency of each level of filters to avoid too large differences in efficiency between adjacent levels, which affects the protective effect of the previous level on the next level.

Consideration of the outdoor environment
Determine dust content: Correctly determining the dust content and dust particle characteristics of outdoor air is an important basis for selecting filters, because the main function of filters is to filter and purify outdoor air and send it indoors.
Filter characteristic evaluation
Confirm filtration efficiency: Different filters have different filtration efficiencies, and it is necessary to select the appropriate efficiency level according to the needs of the clean room.
Calculate resistance and penetration rate: The resistance and penetration rate of the filter are also important reference indicators, which directly affect the use effect and life of the filter.
Consider dust holding capacity: Dust holding capacity refers to the amount of dust that the filter can hold under specific conditions, which determines the service life and replacement frequency of the filter.
Parameter setting Control wind speed and air volume: The setting of filtering wind speed and processing air volume is also very critical, which affects the effect of air circulation and the working performance of the filter.
Layout selection Centralized medium efficiency filter: Medium efficiency air filters should be centrally set in the positive pressure section of the purified air conditioning to optimize their filtration effect. End-set cleanroom filter : HEPA or sub-high efficiency air filters should be set at the end of the purified air conditioning system to ensure the ultimate purification effect.
