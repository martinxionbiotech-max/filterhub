The sole cleaning machine adopts advanced technology and unique brush structure combined cycle water cleaning technology, rapid removal of sole dust particles, the sole can effectively clean.Clean fast, efficient, energy saving and environmental protection.One investment, long-term use, effectively reduce production costs.
Suitable for various entrances, the sole contamination is completely isolated, reliable and effective to ensure the hygienic quality of the enterprise.

Equipment operation:
1. Open the foot pedal to confirm whether the water level is maintained at the MAX scale;
2. Confirm whether the sensor lamp is on after the device is energized;
3. Block the sensor once, and confirm whether the device can work normally and whether the brush works normally;
4. Cancel the blocking sensor and confirm whether the device can stop automatically after about 15 seconds;
5. Press the drainage button to check whether the water can be drained normally.
Preparation before equipment operation:
2. Confirm whether the water level is maintained at the MAX scale and that the water is clean enough;
