## V Bank filter
V-bank air filters serve as a critical intermediate or terminal filtration stage in commercial HVAC and ventilation systems. Designed for high-performance air handling units (AHUs), they efficiently capture fine particulate matter ≥0.5 microns, with filtration efficiency ranging from 65% to 95% across standardized grades.
Performance Advantages:
- Enhanced Airflow Dynamics: The distinctive V-shaped configuration creates larger intake channels, enabling superior air volume handling with lower static pressure drop compared to flat-panel filters.
- Extended Service Life: Deep pleated media packs maximize filter surface area within compact housings, significantly increasing dust-holding capacity and reducing change frequency.
Modular Construction: Multiple filter cells mount in parallel within a single frame, allowing dense media packing without compromising airflow. This space-efficient design supports higher MERV ratings in constrained mechanical spaces.

#### Filtration specification
Advanced V-Bank Air Filter Specifications & Media Technology
Precision Filtration Performance V-bank filters deliver versatile efficiency across F7-F9 (MERV 13-15) and H10-H14 (HEPA) classifications per EN 1822 standards, making them ideal for:
- Critical Pre-Filtration: Primary protection for HEPA/ULPA systems in ISO Class 5-8 cleanrooms
- Commercial HVAC Optimization: Terminal filtration in hospitals, pharma facilities, electronics manufacturing, and food processing plants
- High-Efficiency Solutions: Available up to U15 (ULPA) for semiconductor/life science applications Key Advantage: Modular V-configuration enables 35% higher media density vs. box filters, maintaining low differential pressure while handling 10-25% greater airflow volumes.
Media Science: Polyester vs. Glass Fiber

Why Glass Fiber Dominates Critical Applications:
- Zero Off-Gassing – Eliminates odor risks in sterile environments (ISO 14644 compliant)
- Hydrophobic Structure – Resists microbial growth (validated per IEST-RP-CC001)
- UL 900 Class 1 Fire Rating – Mandatory for labs/hospitals
- Nanofiber Enhancements – Available for sub-micron capture at 0.3-0.5µm
Industry-Specific Configurations

- Data Centers: F7-F9 polyester for cost-effective particulate control
