The pocket filter with very good dust-holding ability, and it is the second filtration of an air handling unit. The multi-pocket design helps enlarge the airflow rate and reduce pressure drop.

### Why are there different colors?
Pocket filter media features various colors primarily to distinguish between different filtration grades for easy identification during selection, installation, and replacement. While there are common color conventions in the industry, custom colors are also available to meet specific user needs.
#### Filtration Grade vs. Color Correspondence Table
TABLE:
  EN 779 Filtration Grade | Corresponding MERV Grade | Common Media Color
  F5 | MERV 11 | White
  F6 | MERV 12 | Green
  F7 | MERV 13 | Pink
  F8 | MERV 14 | Yellow
  F9 | MERV 15 | White

Note: The above colors are industry-standard conventions. Custom colors can be provided based on specific requirements.

## Frequently Asked Questions
A pocket filter is commonly used in air conditioning systems, HVAC, Air handling units, fresh air systems and air handlers to remove dust and other particles from the air before it enters the system. It is also used in industrial processes where clean air is required, such as food processing or pharmaceutical, medical, and micron-electronics. Additionally, pocket filters are used in vacuum cleaners and other household appliances to prevent dust and debris from entering the machine and causing damage.
The lifespan of a pocket filter depends on the quality of airflow into it and the frequency of use. Generally speaking, pocket filters can last anywhere from 6 months to a year. However, it is recommended to replace them every 3-6 months to ensure optimal performance and prevent clogging or damage to the filter. It is also important to add some prefilter before it to remove larger dust that can reduce the burden of pocket filters.
