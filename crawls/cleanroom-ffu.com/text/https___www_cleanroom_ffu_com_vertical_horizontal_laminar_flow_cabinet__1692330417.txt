## Vertical laminar flow cabinet / clean bench
Vertical laminar flow cabinets provide a vertical unidirectional flow of clean airflow from top to bottom. The low-noise centrifugal fan and HEPA Filter are combined into a unified structure. Its use has a good effect on improving process conditions and increasing product output.
