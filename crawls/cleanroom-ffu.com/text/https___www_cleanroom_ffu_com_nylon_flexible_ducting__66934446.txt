## Nylon flexible ducting manufacturer-Small MOQ-Fast delivery
Nylon flexible ducting is one of the commonly used ducts in ventilation systems. It has a stainless steel skeleton on the inside and high-quality nylon cloth on the outside. Specifications of different lengths and inner diameters are provided according to the needs of ventilation ducts. It has the characteristics of corrosion resistance, wide operating temperature range, not easy to wear, easy connection, high shrinkage ratio, etc.
In order to improve the quality of nylon flexible ducting, we control it from the source and select source materials that comply with ROHS standards to ensure environmental protection and no strong odor. The product has strong pressure resistance and elasticity, is not easy to break and deform, is durable and has good ductility, and can be stretched directly by hand.
Made with high-quality glue and stitching technology, it won’t fall off. Excellent design, good flexibility, and bendability, suitable for a variety of complex use environments.
Quality craftsmanship: professional technical craftsmanship, high-standard production and strict control at all levels
As a manufacturer of Nylon flexible ducting, we provide high-quality products to our dealers or end customers. We can provide customers with services such as LOGO printing on the outer nylon cloth, and the same can be done for the outer packaging of the product.
