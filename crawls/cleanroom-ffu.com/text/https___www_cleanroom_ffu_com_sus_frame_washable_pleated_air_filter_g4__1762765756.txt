## SUS frame washable pleated air filter G4
the design pleated air filter efficiency is G4 EN:779, a top-rated air filter for commercial HVAC systems. the stainless steel frame gives a robust construction to the whole filter.
The stainless steel frame provides the extreme ability for anti-rust, anti-alkali, and anti-sour. It has the characteristics of coastal anti-smog corrosion.
the filter media is white synthetic fiber T5-10mm to provide a better dust-holding ability and more average designs on the market. the filter media can be replaced easily thus no need to replace the whole pleated air filter, this brings a better economic option.
The stainless steel frame and inside metal skeleton can let us make a huge size filter, if you are looking to build a quite big pleated air filter for your ventilation system, this is a very reliable design. the inside metal skeleton can prevent filter media from bending even with a large size and high airflow speed.
Eawayfilter is a professional manufacturer for making many different designs of pleated air filters for your ventilation HVAC, AHU, etc. We provide custom sizes and custom efficiency, fast delivery time, and good prices, welcome to contact us.
