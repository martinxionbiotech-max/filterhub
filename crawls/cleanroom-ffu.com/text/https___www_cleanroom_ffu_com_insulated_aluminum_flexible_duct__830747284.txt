## Thermal Insulated aluminum flexible duct for HVAC with air conditioning
Thermally insulated aluminum flexible duct with glass fiber as the intermediate material can be provided, which is crucial in ventilation systems with air conditioning. The built-in glass fiber has a very good thermal insulation effect, is corrosion-resistant, and does not react with the surrounding environment and long-term use.
