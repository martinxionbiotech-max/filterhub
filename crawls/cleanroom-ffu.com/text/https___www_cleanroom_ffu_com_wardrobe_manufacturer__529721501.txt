## Cleanroom wardrobe manufacturer
A cleanroom wardrobe is a kind of special-purpose closet for the cleanroom industry, widely used in medicine, preparation, electronics, precision instruments, meters, and food industries such as special occasions for storing cleanroom garments;
It is a device with a HEPA filter mounted on the top and creates a purified airflow from up to down to protect the garments from air pollution.
Construction
- Stainless steel 201 / 304 / 316 / 316L / Aluminum alloy square tube 40*40mm
- HEPA filter
- PVC vinyl wall or acrylic wall
- Fan filter unit on the ceiling, back centrifugal fan
- Air velocity adjustable
- Cleanliness level class 100
- Holder bar for garments
- Small closet compartment
- Universal wheel with brake
Note: the company can customize the special specification dustless wardrobe according to customer’s requirement.
Optional accessories
- Pressure gauge
- UV light
