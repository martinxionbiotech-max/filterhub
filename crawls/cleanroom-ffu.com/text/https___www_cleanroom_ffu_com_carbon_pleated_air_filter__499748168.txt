## Activated carbon pleated air filter
Carbon pleated air filter is a popular design used in air conditioning ventilation systems and other air filtration equipment and air purifiers. It combines the odor adsorption function of activated carbon with the particle collection capabilities of synthetic fiber to provide a cost-effective air purification solution.
The air filter is usually made of high-quality activated carbon fiber as the filter material and adds wire mesh on the air outlet surface to support it. It can be folded into a pleated V shape by the machine, which can improve the overall structural stability. These materials not only have a good deodorization effect, but also have a long service life, and can maintain efficient filtration performance for a long time. Because of the high content of activated carbon, this filter has a large adsorption area and strong adsorption performance and has a good removal effect on odor and harmful gases in the air.
Standards and customization
Not only do we provide standard-sized carbon pleated air filters, but also customized services. We can choose different frame materials according to customer requirements, such as paper frame, aluminum frame, galvanized frame, stainless steel frame, etc. The thickness and length and width can also be customized according to actual needs. Regular thicknesses include 1 inch (21-25mm), 2 inches (44-46mm), and 4 inches (90-100mm) to meet different air volume and filtration needs.
Low resistance
While maintaining high adsorption performance, the carbon pleated air filter can also maintain low ventilation resistance, which means that efficient filtration can be achieved without sacrificing airflow.
High economy
Carbon pleated air filter have cost advantages in manufacturing and maintenance, making them an affordable choice in many application scenarios.
Widely used
It is suitable for ventilation and air conditioning systems of large civil buildings such as office buildings, conference rooms, hospitals, shopping malls and airports, as well as central air conditioning systems of ordinary industrial plants or clean rooms. activated carbon filter can play the role of chemical filtration to improve indoor air quality (IAQ) and protect people’s health in places where specific gas pollutants need to be removed, such as laboratories and industrial sites where special gases are produced.
