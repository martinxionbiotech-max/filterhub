# Pre & medium filter
Pre & mediumIn the cleanroom ventilation system, the filter is mainly used to initially purify the air, protect the next level of filtering equipment and extend the system. Service life and the like.
#### Pre filter
- Primary purification: Pre filter is mainly used to filter dust particles above 5 microns. As the first line of defense in the air treatment process, it is responsible for capturing larger dust particles and reducing the coarse dust content in the air.
- Protecting the next filter stage: in a multi-stage filtration system, The existence of the primary filter effectively lightens the burden of subsequent filters (such as medium-efficiency and high-efficiency filters), Avoid premature clogging of these precision filters and ensure their normal operation.
- The service life is prolonged: because the primary filter is used for capturing a large amount of large particles, The downstream filtering equipment is protected, thereby prolonging the service life of the equipment.
- Maintenance of ventilation system performance: by removing large foreign matter,The primary filter ensures that the performance of the ventilation system is not degraded by the accumulation of dust.
- Convenient replacement: As the primary filter is usually designed in plate, folding, bag and other styles, They generally have large filtration area and strong dust holding capacity, and the replacement cycle is usually three to four months. This depends on the air quality at the place of use.
#### Medium efficiency filter
- Secondary purification: A medium efficiency filter is used to capture 1-5 micron dust particles and various suspended solids. In the filtration system, it undertakes the task of further purifying the air.
- Pre-filter: medium efficiency filter is usually used as the pre-filter of high efficiency filter. It protects the HEPA filter from smaller particles, Make sure the HEPA filter can focus on the finer particles.
- Industrial applications: In the industrial field, the medium efficiency filter can also be used for general air purification.For example, the intermediate filtration of the central air conditioning system and the places with high dust content.
- Long replacement cycle: As the accuracy of the medium efficiency filter is higher than that of the initial efficiency filter, its replacement cycle is relatively long. Usually 6 to 12 months.
