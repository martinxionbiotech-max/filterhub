## High volume Multi-wedge HEPA filter
High volume Multi-wedge HEPA filter has a better airflow rate and lower resistance, when comparing with the traditional panel HEPA filters. The innovative (V-type) structure: after processing the filter media through minipleat folding, the V-shaped structure is placed inside the cabinet and fixed with sheet metal and sealant, creating multiple parallel filtration units within a confined space.
The advantage of this (V-type) design is particularly prominent under industry-standard dimensions. Such as the two mainstream installation sizes—24x24x12 (inches) and 12x24x12 (inches), the V-Type structure can increase the filtration area to 3-5 times that of flat-panel HEPA filters of the same size. Specifically, the 24x24x12-inch V-Type High Volume HEPA filter can achieve a filtration area exceeding 10 square meters, while the 12x24x12-inch model reaches 5-8 square meters.
A larger filtration area means a significant increase in air throughput per unit time. Meanwhile, the air flow in the V-Type channels is more uniform and gentle, effectively reducing the resistance when air passes through the filter media. Typically, its initial resistance can be controlled at 120-150Pa, much lower than the over 200Pa of flat-panel HEPA filters with the same air volume, achieving an optimized balance between air volume and resistance.

## Product Design Features
#### High-Efficiency Filtration and Perfect Sealing Design
High efficiency is the core of the V-type High Volume HEPA filter. The filter media is made of ultra-fine glass fiber or PTFE materials, which feature fine fiber diameter, uniform pore distribution, and high interception efficiency, effectively capturing fine particles of 0.3μm and below.
To ensure stable output of high-efficiency performance, the High Volume HEPA filter is sealed to the cabinet using AB two-component polyurethane adhesive. The mating surface between the frame and the cabinet is equipped with elastic gaskets (EVA, Neoprene, PU) or adopts Gel Seal technology, with sealing performance meeting the stringent requirements of EN 1822 standard.
Through these designs, it can stably achieve multiple high-efficiency grades: H13, H14, and U15. The H13 grade has a filtration efficiency of ≥99.97% for 0.3μm particles, the H14 grade ≥99.995%, and the U15 grade as high as ≥99.9995%, fully meeting the air cleanliness requirements of scenarios such as biosafety laboratories and sterile pharmaceutical workshops.
#### Flexible Cabinet Structure
Installation environments and interface requirements vary significantly across different industries and purification systems. We provide diversified cabinet designs for the V-type Multi-wedge HEPA filter to fully adapt to complex scenarios. The cabinet is made of high-strength galvanized steel sheet or stainless steel , processed through precision technologies such as stamping, bending, and riveting. It is lightweight, high-strength, and corrosion-resistant , suitable for special working conditions such as humidity and dust.

#### 4V, 5V, 6V Multi-Channel Specifications
We offer multiple channel quantity specifications (4V, 5V, 6V, etc.) for the High Volume V-type HEPA filter, achieving precise matching between air volume and cost. The core impact of channel quantity lies in filtration area: taking the 24x24x12-inch High Volume HEPA filter as an example, the 4V design provides a filtration area of approximately 10 square meters, the 5V design increases to 12-13 square meters, and the 6V design reaches over 15 square meters, with corresponding rated air volumes of about 3000m³/h, 3800m³/h, and 4500m³/h respectively.
This graded design allows users to select the appropriate channel specification based on actual working conditions. For scenarios with moderate air volume requirements such as small and medium-sized clean workshops, the 4V or 5V specification is sufficient with better cost advantages. For high-air-volume demand scenarios such as semiconductor wafer manufacturing and large-scale bioreactor workshops, the 6V specification or multiple units in parallel can be used to ensure sufficient air volume.
## Application
#### Electronics industry & semiconductor
In the electronics industry, semiconductor chip manufacturing is extremely sensitive to impurities such as dust particles and metal ions in the air. The H14 grade can effectively intercept these impurities, avoiding chip short circuits or performance degradation.
#### Pharmaceutical & hospital operating rooms
In the pharmaceutical industry, GMP workshops for sterile drug production require air cleanliness to reach Class 100 or Class 1000. It can be used as terminal filtration equipment to ensure a sterile and dust-free production environment and guarantee drug quality.
#### Food processing & biological laboratories
In the food processing industry, scenarios such as baking and dairy production need to prevent microbial contamination and cross-contamination of dust. It can effectively filter bacteria and dust in the air, extending the shelf life of food.
## Typical Features
#### Low Resistance and Energy Saving
The energy consumption of ventilation systems accounts for 30%-50% of the total energy consumption of clean workshops, and the resistance of High Volume HEPA filters is a core factor affecting fan energy consumption. Thanks to the large filtration area brought by the multi-channel V-Type structure, the initial resistance is only 60%-70% of that of traditional flat-panel HEPA filters, and the resistance rises slowly. Taking a High Volume HEPA filter with a rated air volume of 3000m³/h as an example, the initial resistance of a traditional flat-panel HEPA filter is about 220Pa, while that of the V-Type is only 130Pa. According to the fan power calculation formula P=Q×ΔP/(3600×η) (where Q is air volume, ΔP is resistance, and η is fan efficiency), with a fan efficiency of 70%, the fan power corresponding to the V-Type can be reduced by about 40%.
#### Long Service Life and Cost Savings
The long service life of the High Volume HEPA filter is another important embodiment of its cost reduction advantage. On one hand, the large filtration area significantly increases the dust-holding capacity of the filter media—compared with flat-panel HEPA filters of the same grade, the dust-holding capacity of the High Volume HEPA filter is 2-3 times higher, and the replacement cycle can be extended to 24-36 months in environments with the same dust concentration.
#### Reduced replacement frequency
On the other hand, reduced replacement frequency can effectively reduce downtime losses and labor costs. The replacement of HEPA filters in clean workshops usually requires shutdown, and the downtime loss in industries such as semiconductors and pharmaceuticals is extremely high, reaching tens of thousands or even hundreds of thousands of yuan per hour. The V-Type reduces replacement frequency, avoiding huge production losses caused by shutdown. Meanwhile, each replacement requires 2-3 professional personnel to spend several hours, and extending the replacement cycle can significantly reduce labor input, further optimizing the comprehensive operating costs of enterprises.
