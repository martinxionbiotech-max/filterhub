## Ducted Ceiling-mounted HEPA Filter Diffuser
the HEPA filter diffuser is a terminal stage filtration equipment in a clean room, it is made of a housing, filter pack, collar, regular valve, and heat insulation.
Disposable HEPA Filter Diffuser Module Box
The disposable HEPA Filter diffuser, HEPA filters cannot be replaceable, once the final air pressure drop is reached. The whole module should be replaced.
It connects with the air pipeline directly and is sealed with good air-tight performance. Very nice air purifying ability, it`s very suitable for class 10000 to class 100 laminar flow or turbulence flow cleanroom.
Small size and lightweight, with anodized aluminum alloy as the side frame, and standard thickness is 150m or 180mm, their different air inlet size is 10, 12, and 14 inches.

Ducted HEPA filter with collar
The HEPA filter diffuser can be quickly installed in the cleanroom and directly connected to the air supply duct.
A rectangular or round collar is mounted on one side of the housing. The collar will connect with the duct directly, so HEPA is very easy to directly connect to the pipe system.
Perfect seal
The HEPA filter material is wrapped with a metal housing, and sealed with polyurethane glue on every four sides.
Installation
Usually, this type of HEPA diffuser will be installed in the top ceiling of a cleanroom, which can generate a clean airflow from up to down.
Also, it can be installed on the side wall to generate a horizontal clean airflow.
Need to confirm has enough space to install the diffuser on the top ceiling or side wall.
