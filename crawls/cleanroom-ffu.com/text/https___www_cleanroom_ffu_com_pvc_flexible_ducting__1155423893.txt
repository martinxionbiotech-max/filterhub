## PVC flexible ducting Manufacturer
PVC flexible ducting is a new type of ventilation system HVAC ducting. Compared with traditional aluminum foil ducting, it is fireproof, waterproof, anti-virus, durable, and simple to make. The fabric material structure is durable, wear-resistant, and not easy to break. In many cases, it can be used as an alternative to aluminum foil piping.
Its surface fibers are coated with PVC, PTFE, flame-retardant silicone and other refractory materials.
PVC flexible ducting has a variety of colors to choose from, and the color does not affect the effect of use, usually green and yellow, orange, black, etc. The built-in skeleton is made of stainless steel, with counterclockwise threads, and there is a metal protective ring on the outside to extend the service life of the product and prevent scratches.
Use galvanized iron or stainless steel connectors to facilitate the connection of two different pipelines.
Features
- Fireproof, waterproof, antivirus
- Durable
- Multiple pipe connections
- Built-in stainless steel ring
- Metal exoskeleton
- Multiple colours green, orange, green, gray, blue
- Thick material 0.5mm
- Maximum pressure resistance: 5000Pa
- Temperature range: -30 degrees Celsius – +200 degrees Celsius
- Dimensions: Inner diameter 80MM–1500MM
- Shrinkage ratio: 10:1
Applications
Various machinery, smoke, dust, high-temperature gas factory soldering fume, blast furnace and welding gas exhaust emissions.
