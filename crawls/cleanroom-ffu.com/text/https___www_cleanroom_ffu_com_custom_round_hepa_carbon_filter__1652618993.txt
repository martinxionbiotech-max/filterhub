## Custom around HEPA filter
#### Custom round HEPA filter – Sleeve Design
We offer customization in various dimensions, including inner diameter , outer diameter , height , and filtration grade , to meet your diverse application needs. We also provide the most cost-effective manufacturing method based on your required quantity.
We can produce HEPA-grade round air filters with efficiencies reaching H13 (99.95%) and H14 (99.995%) @ 0.3μm. They are suitable for various places requiring high air cleanliness, such as medical equipment, air purifiers, waste gas treatment units, and so on.
Most round HEPA filters require special mold opening for their end caps, such as plastic or metal parts. For some non-standard sizes , it may be impossible to directly find suitable end caps. We can provide 3D-printed end caps to support your small-batch customization or initial product development smoothly.

#### How to custom round HEPA filter – sleeve design
First, please inform us of the product dimensions, including inner diameter , outer diameter , height , air flow direction , filtration grade , whether activated carbon is needed for smoke odor removal, and whether a pre-filter layer is required.
Second, please specify the quantity of products. This allows us to select the most suitable and cost-effective customization method for you. For small quantities, 3D-printed end caps or non-woven fabric end caps can be chosen. For large-quantity purchases, custom-molded plastic or metal end caps are available.
Regarding the sealing method: for high-grade HEPA efficiency, we recommend using Polyurethane as the sealant. For low-grade efficiency, hot-melt adhesive can be used.
Before production, we will design a 3D drawing for you to ensure every detail is correct. More details can be obtained through communication with our professional technical team. Please feel free to share your ideas and requirements as thoroughly as possible.

#### Inner metal grille
Its main purpose is to provide better structural strength for the round HEPA filter. Since the filter media is relatively soft, it ensures the filter media will not be crushed when the filter needs to be compressed to achieve a better sealing effect.Generally, a minimum order quantity (MOQ) of 50 units or more is required to implement this. Customization of the inner metal grille is not feasible for orders with too few quantities.
#### EVA foam gasket
The sealing gasket is usually placed on the top or bottom, though there are also cases where it needs to be installed on the side. Common types include EVA (Neoprene), foam, PU, and EPDM sealing gaskets. When selecting a sealing gasket, you can inform us of the required elastic deformation range to ensure the filter can be installed tightly without being over-compressed.
#### Nylon mesh layer
Its main function is to block fine dust, flocculent substances, and hair floating in the air from entering the narrow spaces of the filter media. This is highly beneficial for extending the service life of the round HEPA filter, as the collected debris attached to the surface of the nylon mesh can be cleaned regularly. Similarly, a layer of polyester filter media pad can be added on the outer side to achieve primary filtration.

#### Custom round HEPA filter – flat design
Round HEPA filters are quite common in fresh air systems . They are also used in special circular designs required for medical equipment, dust removal devices, air purifiers , and air scrubbers .
We provide HEPA-grade round air filters , and can also offer odor removal filters with activated carbon particles as well as chemical filters—these can be either combined into one unit or supplied separately.
In the market, round HEPA filters have no standard sizes except for replacement filters for most existing equipment. Therefore, they are custom-made in most cases. We use paperboard or non-woven fabric for the frame and PTFE as the filtering material. We can customize various diameters and thicknesses to meet the requirements of different filtration grades.

#### How to custom flat round HEPA filter
Similarly, you need to inform us of some basic parameters, such as diameter , thickness , filtration grade , and required quantity . Our Flat Design Round HEPA Filter always meets HEPA-grade filtration requirements.
It can be composed of various multi-functional composite materials. For example, activated carbon particles can be added to remove odors, an antibacterial layer to prevent bacterial growth, and primary-effect sponge, non-woven fabric, or nylon mesh layers to achieve multi-stage filtration.
We will select the appropriate manufacturing plan based on your required quantity. The production process is mainly distinguished by the different frame materials—we commonly use paperboard, non-woven fabric, custom-molded plastic parts, and 3D-printed parts.
The filter material is PTFE synthetic fiber, which is machine-folded into a pleated structure and then cut into a circular shape using a die cutter or laser machine.
