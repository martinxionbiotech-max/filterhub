##### Separator HEPA Filter for Clean Room
## What is Separator HEPA Filter for Clean Room?
the Separator HEPA Filter for Clean Room is an air filter used in cleanrooms. Separator means a spacer between filter media, there are aluminum foil or paper separators. two designs available. the paper separator can work in a dry situation and the price is good. the aluminum foil separator can work in a humidity situation MAX 80%. but the price is a little bit higher than the paper separator design.
## Need to be careful when handling a separator HEPA filter
Because the separator & glass fiber media are quite fragile, please be careful when dealing with it.
A protective mesh can be used to protect the filter media and separator.
## A polyurethane glue creates a perfect seal
To ensure no any leakage of the separator HEPA filter , the filter media and housing are perfect sealed by AB component polyurethane glue.
this glue is liquid firstly, and then will dry within 24 hours.
A good quality separator HEPA filter should has 4 sides with enough glue sealed. ( Up, down, left side, right side )
