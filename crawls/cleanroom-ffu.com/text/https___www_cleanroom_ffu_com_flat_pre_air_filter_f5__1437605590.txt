## Metal Frame Flat pre air filter F5
this air filter is made of metal frame and flat synthetic media. the efficiency is G4 and F5 According to EN:779. Considering the thickness of media, the filter comes with three common thickness which is 20, 25, 45mm.
Compared with a pleated pre air filter , the advantage of this filter is it has an extremely good dust-collection ability cause the filter media is fluffy. the media will collect dust then the filter media becomes dark.
The filter media is fixed by two sides metallic mesh grille, on upstream side the grille can be removed when replace a filter media is needed. This feature can let users save their costs because don`t need to replace the whole filter.
The airflow rate will be a little lower than the normal pleated design, the main function of this filter is to collect large-sized dust and paint particles.
