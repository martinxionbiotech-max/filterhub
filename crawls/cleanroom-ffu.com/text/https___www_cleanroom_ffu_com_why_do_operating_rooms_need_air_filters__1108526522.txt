##### Why do operating rooms need air filters?
As an important place for surgery in the hospital, the operating room has a high requirement for the cleanliness of the environment, because the body resistance of the patient is weak during the surgery, and the surgical incision is prone to infection. Therefore, in order to reduce the risk of infection during surgery and ensure the safety of patients and the success of surgery, the operating room needs to purify the air through air filters.
## Decontamination requirements
The operating room needs to be decontaminated to ensure air quality during surgery.
Air filters can effectively remove microorganisms and dust from the air, reducing the risk of infection.
Formation of stream-like air flow: The stream-like air flow formed by the laminar flow hood can pass through the surgical area at a uniform speed, so that indoor microorganisms and dust particles are effectively discharged to avoid indoor diffusion. This mechanism ensures that the air within the surgical area is dynamically clean, reducing the risk of cross-infection.
## Improve surgical success rate
A clean operating environment helps to improve the success rate of surgery and the speed of patient recovery.
The use of filters provides a better working environment for doctors, thus improving the efficiency and quality of surgery.
## Prevent cross infection
Air filters prevent harmful outdoor particles from entering the operating room and prevent cross-infection.
The risk of infection is further reduced through strict control of indoor temperature and humidity and airflow distribution.
## Stratified flow design
The stratified flow design ensures that the air flows in a parallel flow line, uniform flow rate and single direction, avoiding dust floating and improving cleanliness.
The vertical laminar flow design helps to maximize air filtration and purification.
## The development of medical technology
With the development of medical technology, clean operating room has become one of the most important and highly technical departments in hospital.
The use of high efficiency filters and other equipment reflects the high requirements of modern medical facilities for a clean environment.
## Protection of medical personnel
The clean operating environment not only protects the patient, but also protects the staff from infection.
A good working environment helps to improve the job satisfaction and efficiency of medical staff.
