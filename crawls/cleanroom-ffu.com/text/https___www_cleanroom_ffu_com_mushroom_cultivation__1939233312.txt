## Mushroom Cultivation Air Purification
We offer a variety of air filtration products for mushroom cultivation , providing a better cultivation environment and preventing any form of cross-contamination.
These products can be used to ensure the control and optimization of air quality during mushroom cultivation, including air filters and simply assembled Laminar flow hoods and fan powered HEPA filter .
Fan-powered HEPA filter for mushroom cultivation
#### Fan powered HEPA filter for Mushroom Cultivation
The Fan powered HEPA filter is essentially an industrial-grade air purification equipment, featuring a core configuration of a high-pressure fan + HEPA (High-Efficiency Particulate Air) filter .
Its key performance indicator is the ability to filter air particles ≥ 0.3 microns with a filtration efficiency of over 99.97%. Contaminant fungi (such as Penicillium and Aspergillus) and their spores that need to be controlled in mushroom cultivation typically have a diameter of 2-10 microns, much larger than the filtration precision of fan-powered HEPA filter. Thus, it can completely remove these core pollution sources, while also filtering dust, insect eggs, and other contaminants, fully meeting the purification needs of the cultivation environment.
The dedicated fan equipped with industrial fan-powered HEPA filter provides stable air flow, with the air velocity at the air outlet complying with industrial standards (usually 0.3-0.6 m/s , and up to 0.8 m/s for some high-airflow models). It can form a uniform horizontal or vertical clean air layer in the cultivation area (such as above inoculation tables and cultivation racks), effectively isolating the intrusion of external polluted air and constructing a local clean environment for key links in mushroom cultivation, such as mycelium growth and inoculation.
##### Practical Advantages
- Easy Operation : No complex installation is required. It can be used immediately by connecting to a power source and placing it on a workbench or the top of a cultivation rack. A simple baffle (such as a stainless steel plate or plastic plate) can be installed on the top to prevent dust accumulation on the equipment itself from falling and contaminating the cultivation environment.
- Long Service Life : Matching with a primary filter (to pre-filter large particle dust) can significantly extend the service life of the HEPA filter element, with an overall service life of 3-5 years , adapting to the long-term use needs of large-scale mushroom cultivation.

## Frequently Asked Questions
Prevent cross-contamination During mushroom cultivation, high-efficiency particulate air (HEPA) filters are used to prevent cross-contamination, effectively removing tiny particles in the air, including bacteria, mold spores and other potential pollutants, thus providing a clean environment for mushroom growth. HEPA filters rated above H13 are capable of capturing particles above 0.3 microns with 99.97% efficiency. This means that almost all airborne mold spores and bacteria are effectively intercepted, thus preventing them from entering the environment where mushrooms grow. Create one-way airflow The HEPA Filter is blown by a fan to create a clean, laminar airflow that ensures that uncontaminated clean air flows to the mushroom growth area and prevents outside air with bacteria and other contaminants from entering the inoculation area. Pressure control By blowing the HEPA Filter through the fan, the air pressure in the laminar flow area will be greater than the external pressure, which also prevents the external air with bacteria and other pollution sources from entering the inoculation area. The speed of the fan can be adjusted by the experience of the operator to create different pressure differences. In addition, baffles or PVC curtains can be added around the clean area to reduce the speed of the clean airflow to increase the internal pressure. Sterilization technology A HEPA filter with a germicidal function can be provided so that bacteria and fungi are killed.
Fan-powered HEPA filters will continuously blow out high-purity airflow, and mushroom inoculation can be completed well in this high-purity environment. Please choose H13 or H14 efficiency grade to ensure more than 99.5% of the pollution is removed.
