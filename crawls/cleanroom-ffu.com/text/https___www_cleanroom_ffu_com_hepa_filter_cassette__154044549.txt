#### Typical Application
Deep Pleat HEPA filters are the workhorses of critical air filtration, designed where superior performance, long service life, and reliability are non-negotiable. Their high dust-holding capacity and consistent efficiency make them ideal for a wide range of demanding sectors.
1. Pharmaceutical & Biotechnology Manufacturing In sterile environments where product purity and patient safety are paramount, our Deep Pleat HEPA filters are indispensable. They provide the absolute air cleanliness required in filling rooms, sterile corridors, and aseptic processing areas, ensuring compliance with strict cGMP and regulatory standards by effectively removing airborne contaminants and microorganisms.
2. Microelectronics & Semiconductor Production The manufacture of semiconductors, circuit boards, and microchips demands an environment virtually free of microscopic particles. A single speck of dust can cause a catastrophic defect. Our filters protect sensitive components and costly lithography equipment in cleanrooms, preventing yield loss and ensuring the integrity of minute circuitry.
3. Healthcare & Hospital Isolation Rooms Safeguarding patients and staff is the top priority. Used in HVAC systems for isolation rooms, operating theaters, and immunosuppressed patient wards, these filters capture airborne pathogens, including bacteria, viruses, and fungal spores, helping to prevent cross-contamination and the spread of infectious diseases.
4. Aerospace & Precision Engineering From the assembly of satellite components to the painting of aircraft, controlling particulate contamination is critical. Our filters ensure that finishes are flawless and that highly sensitive mechanical and optical systems are assembled in a pristine environment, free from abrasive or interfering particles.
5. High-Containment Laboratories (BSL-3/4) In facilities handling dangerous biological agents, containing the threat within the laboratory is a matter of public safety. Deep Pleat HEPA filters are a critical engineering control in exhaust and supply air systems, preventing the release of hazardous aerosols into the external environment.
6. Food & Beverage Processing In sectors like dairy, ready-to-eat meals, and beverage filling, maintaining impeccable air quality prevents microbial spoilage and ensures product shelf life. Our filters help achieve the stringent hygiene levels required, removing airborne yeast, mold spores, and bacteria that can compromise product quality and safety.
7. Data Centers & Server Rooms The sensitive electronics in servers generate heat and are susceptible to failure from particulate buildup and corrosive gases. By providing ultra-clean, conditioned air, our filters protect critical IT infrastructure from dust-related overheating and damage, enhancing system reliability and uptime.
8. General Industrial & Heavy-Duty Ventilation In settings like chemical processing, paint spray booths, or welding areas, these filters handle high particulate loads from industrial processes. Their deep-pleat design provides an extended service life even in challenging conditions, protecting equipment and improving indoor air quality for personnel.
## Construction design
##### Aluminum separators or paper separators
because the is used in normal temperatures, it is not necessary to consider the use conditions in high-temperature environments. We usually have two designs of separator paper.
Aluminum separators are more durable and resistant to corrosion and moisture, but the cost is relatively higher.
On the other hand, The price of paper separators is relatively cheap. While, it can not be used in humid environments, and it may become a breeding ground for microorganisms, so in the case of considering the cost, special attention should be paid to the use scenario of the filter.
We recommend using paper separators in dry conditions so that the performance of the filter is not affected in any way.
##### A faceguard of the filter
Cause the filter media glass fiber is quite fragile, any mistake handling will cause damage to the media. to protect the filter media we recommend adding a faceguard on both sides.
the faceguard is metal or powder coated, which is quite strong and can protect filter media well.

## FAQ
HEPA filter cassette is also called deep pleat HEPA filter, the deep pleat design means the filter media pack has a thickness of at least 6inch (150mm), and even thicker such as 10inch (220mm), or 12inch (292mm). Compared with the panel HEPA filter , the filter media is much thicker, that why called “Deep”. Also, because the filter media is pleated, that is why people call it “ Deep pleat “. The deep pleat HEPA filter comes with separators , normally, there are aluminum foil or paper separators. When you purchase this filter, it is very crucial to confirm the separator material with your supplier cause the effect final price and filter performance. There is at least a 30% price difference between these two designs. Advantages of aluminum separator HEPA filter for some applications such as pharma and food processes, an aluminum foil separator design is necessary. Cause the paper separator is an organic material that may breed fungus and mold, contaminating the ventilation system. Paper separator HEPA filter has the advantage of low cost, It is widely used in automobile spraying and other precision machinery industries. It can be designed as F8 and H10 with an efficiency of 99.9% for particles of 0.5 microns.
Yes, this is available, the HEPA filter cassette can be designed with a housing and ducted, the housing material is powder-coated metal or stainless steel, the HEPA filter can installed in the housing and replaced easily, housing is ducted with a round or square collar that helps connected with the ventilation duct easily. Also, it can built with a DOP test system , pressure gauge monitor, and even a fan .
It is recommended to replace the HEPA filter cassette every 3 – 5 years, the initial resistance of is HEPA filter is 220Pa (H13) and 230Pa (H14), at the air speed of 0. 75m/s (150mm thickness), 1.12m/s(220mm thickness) and 1.5m/s(292mm thickness). The final resistance of this HEPA filter is 600Pa, when the final resistance is reached, a replacement is necessary. * What is the final resistance of a HEPA filter? —– It is the resistance of airflow passing through the HEPA filter pack, when the HEPA filter collects enough pollutants the media is blocked bringing a higher resistance than initially. Be careful! Failure to replace the HEPA filter Cassette in time will increase the burden on the fan and thus increase power consumption, and the airflow may break through the HEPA, causing pollutants to enter the equipment and cause pollution.
It is not available to wash the HEPA filter cassette, this filter is disposable. the filter media will be broken when washing it. Also, cannot wash the dust out of the glassfiber media.
