#### Best and Most Economical Solution for a large size room
Billiard rooms, Internet cafes, bars, and restaurants usually have a lot of people smoking, as long as more than four people are smoking, then the whole place will appear to be surrounded by second-hand smoke.
This can make other non-smokers feel uncomfortable. To enhance the experience of other users, you need to buy some air purifiers.
Most of the air purifiers on the market are small and are used in small rooms such as bedrooms and study rooms. Not suitable for large places, or you need to buy a multi-unit air purifier to fix the lack of small air purifiers. This will make the management unchanged and add excessive costs.
Our air purifier is converted from an industrial FFU. Its ventilation volume is very large, and one can meet the air purification needs of 50-100m2 rooms. It uses a large air filter composed of glass fiber and activated carbon, which gives it a wonderful performance for removing smoke and odor. Compared with the common air purifier filter elements on the market, they are built with small PTFE HEPA, which can effectively remove 0.3-micron smoke particles, but PTFE is not as stable and durable as glass fiber. Using our retrofit products can make you don`t have to replace air filters so often.
Of course, you can buy some other large size industrial or commercial air purifiers, but their price is very high and even the replacement air filter is expansive. To save money, why not to try our retrofit FFU air purifier?
