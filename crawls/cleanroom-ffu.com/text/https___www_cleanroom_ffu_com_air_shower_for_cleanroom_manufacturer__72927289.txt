Why choose a sliding door?
Siding door air showers are widely used in busy cleanroom workshops.

## FAQ
According to international standards, clean rooms can be classified as ISO 1 to ISO 9, with ISO 5 (or higher, such as ISO 4, ISO 3, etc.) usually requiring the use of air showers. ISO 1 to ISO 8 cleanrooms: These cleanrooms have relatively low contamination control requirements, so air showers may not be required. These rooms are typically used for less sensitive operations such as packaging, assembly, and some manufacturing processes. In these environments, lower air quality standards are sufficient to meet production needs. ISO 5 cleanrooms: Air showers become particularly important in ISO 5 or higher cleanrooms. These environments are often used for the production of high-precision electronic products, the manufacture of some medical equipment, laboratory research, etc., and have strict control requirements for the number of particles and microorganisms in the air. The application of air shower in this level of clean room ensures that the pollution load carried by personnel can be greatly reduced before entering, and the pollutants can be prevented from entering the clean area. ISO 4 and above: For clean rooms with ISO 4 and above, air showers are almost necessary. These levels of cleanrooms are used for extremely sensitive processes such as semiconductor manufacturing, aseptic handling of biopharmaceuticals, and certain biosafety applications. In these cases, air showers not only come as standard, but are often equipped with more advanced air filtration and purification technologies to ensure extremely low pollution levels.
Regularly check and replace the air filter One of the core functions of an air shower is to remove particles from the air through a high-efficiency filter. Therefore, it is necessary to regularly check the status of filters and replace clogged or damaged filters in time to maintain air quality and flow. Clean internal surfaces The surfaces inside the air shower should be cleaned regularly to prevent the accumulation of dust and dirt. Proper cleaning agents and tools such as anti-static cloths can be used to wipe down walls, floors, and ceilings to reduce the deposition of contaminants. Interlock doors and weatherstrip Doors and weatherstrips are an important part of an air shower and need to be checked regularly for wear or damage. Any crack or damage may affect the sealing effect of the air shower, thus affecting its performance. Ensure that all seals are intact and that the door closes and opens properly. Automatic sensors and control system Automatic sensors and control systems are essential for the automated operation of the air shower. Check and test the function of these components regularly to ensure that they work correctly. Airspeed & noise By monitoring the performance of the air shower, including wind speed, noise level, and energy consumption, potential problems can be identified early and action taken. Keep a log of air shower operations to record maintenance and inspections to help track equipment status and manage maintenance cycles. Training Make sure that all employees who use the air shower know how to use it properly and why it needs maintenance. Provide appropriate training and updates to ensure proper use of the air shower by each employee.
