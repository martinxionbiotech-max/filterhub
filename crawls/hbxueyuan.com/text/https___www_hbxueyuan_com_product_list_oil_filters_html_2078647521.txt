## Function of the oil filter
Oil filter is used to remove dust, metal particles, carbon sediment and soot particles and other impurities in the oil to protect the engine.
## Working Principle
In the process of engine operation, with the operation of the oil pump, the oil with impurities from the oil filter bottom assembly of the oil inlet into the oil filter, through the check valve to the outside of the filter paper waiting for filtering, under the action of oil pressure, oil continuously through the filter paper into the central pipe, then thempurities in the oil are filtered on filter paper .
## Features and Benefits
High quality oil filter paper is microporous paper filter paper treated by resin. It can filter impurities under severe temperature changes, ensure sufficient flow, have strong filtration ability, small flow resistance, long service life and other properties. When the engine is working, metal debris, dust, high temperature oxidized carbon and gelatinous sediment, water, etc. are constantly mixed with lubricating oil. The function of oil filter is to filter out these mechanical impurities and gum, keep the lubricating oil clean, protect the engine, prolong its service life .
## Oil filter performance requirements
A: Sealing test: assembly under 0.8mpa oil pressure, holding pressure for 5 minutes, oil filter each seal, not allow leakage.
B. Static pressure test of the assembly: the assembly shall be kept under 1.5mpa oil pressure for 5 minutes, and leakage shall not be allowed at each seal of the oil filter, and the product shall not be damaged.
C. Assembly pulse fatigue test: oil seal in 0-0.8mpa oil pressure, 20 times per minute, 15,000 times, assembly seals are not allowed to leak, the product is not damaged.
D. Assembly resistance to high oil temperature test: at 135°C oil temperature, 192 hours, filter element is not allowed to damage, off the cover, filter paper is not allowed to damage.
E. High pressure drop test of filter element: under 0.35mpa oil pressure, filter element is not allowed to be damaged or deformed.
F. Opening pressure of bypass valve: it shall meet the requirements of drawings.
G. Tightness of bypass valve: the actual opening pressure of the bypass valve is reduced by 30Kpa, and the oil leakage does not exceed 0.2L/min.
H. Passability of bypass valve: under rated flow, the pressure of bypass valve is not more than 200Kpa.
I. Check valve opening pressure: shall meet the requirements of the drawings.
J. Check valve sealing: under 600mm oil column, not more than 0.1L for 6 hours.
K. Assembly original resistance: under rated flow, the assembly original resistance is not more than 25Kpa.
L. Assembly original filtration efficiency: not less than 60% (by weight).
M. Assembly plugging life: when resistance is 70Kpa, the plugging life of the laboratory is not less than 3 hours.
N. Assembly internal cleanliness: calculate according to the rated flow of the assembly. When the rated flow is less than 10L/min, the assembly internal cleanliness is 3mg/min. When the rated flow is greater than 10L/min, the assembly internal cleanliness is 2.8mg/min.

## What happens if I change the oil but not the oil filter?
Can I change the oil without changing the filter? Yes, you can, but the engine oil filter manufacturer advises that this is usually not a good idea. This oil will not drain due to the anti-reflux valve on the filter. If you do not change the filter, this old, dirty oil will mix with your fresh oil.
## What happens if we don't change the oil filter?
The oil filter traps dirt, sludge, metal particles and other contaminants in the oil. This is to keep the oil as clean and pure as possible so that it can lubricate the internal parts of the engine to the maximum extent possible. Without a good filter, slime can damage internal engine components, increasing engine operating temperatures and shortening engine life through increased friction.
Most people change their oil according to the vehicle manufacturer's recommendations in the owner's manual. This can be 5,000 miles, 7,500 miles or other figures. You will find that people are keen to change the oil every 3,000 miles - to ensure it is the cleanest, purest and best it can be.
But many owners and mechanics - many of whom don't go beyond 3,000 miles per oil change - would never dream of changing the oil without changing the filter either. Their reasoning: why put new, clean oil through a filter that has already taken the gunk out of old, dirty oil?
## Frequently Asked Questions about Car Oil Filters
Car Oil Filter FAQWhat are the symptoms of a faulty oil filter? Symptoms of a clogged oil filter: poor performance; there can be many reasons for poor performance, and a clogged oil filter is one of them. Engine sputterer. Audible metallic noise. Low oil pressure. Very dirty exhaust air. What happens if I over-tighten the oil filter? Unfortunately, over-tightening can also cause damage. If you overtighten the drain plug, you may strip the threads. Although it's harder to do, if you do overtighten it, you could damage the rubber gasket on top of the oil filter. Frequently asked questions about car oil filters Can you hand tighten the oil filter? Every reputable oil filter is designed to seal for tens of thousands of miles and only needs to be tightened by hand. You don't need a spanner unless you have a deeply recessed filter with no room around it for your hand... Then use a spanner to tighten it another half turn. Car Oil Filter FAQ Should you pre-fill the oil filter? An age-old question is whether we should pre-fill a new oil filter before installing it. A: If you have a large heavy duty truck, pre-filing will make the process easier, but if it is just a small light vehicle, it is not necessary. Car Oil Filters FAQ Can I use the same oil filter twice? Yes, it is perfectly safe to use any kind of filter with any kind of oil. However, if you practice extended oil change intervals with synthetic oils, a conventional oil filter may not provide the required service life, which means you have to change it in the middle of the oil change cycle, which is inconvenient.
