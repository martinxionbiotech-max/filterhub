# Cabin Air Filter Manufacturer
Pollen, soot, fine dust, allergens and gases are important risk factors for human health and safety in road transport. They can significantly affect the function of heating and air conditioning systems. With our high-quality micronAir cabin air filter solutions, we provide reliable and long-lasting protection for vehicle occupants and system processes.
## Cabin Air Filters
Xueyuan supplies activated carbon to manufacturers of cabin air filters. These filters are found in cars and are used to clean the air entering the car from the outside. Our high quality activated carbon helps clean the air by removing noxious odors and various other unpleasant/harmful substances that can cause lung pressure. Our carbon products can help manufacturers comply with stringent cabin air quality requirements. We have a long history of working with cabin air filter suppliers and our carbon experts know the industry inside and out, meaning we can advise and supply the best carbon for your needs. The cabin air filter is part of the vehicle ventilation system and is located in the external air intake of the passenger compartment in front of the air conditioning system. Typically, the vehicle has a filter media (non-woven) and an activated carbon layer. It is used to purify the air, which is drawn directly from the outside/road and then delivered to the interior of the vehicle via ventilation or air conditioning. Our carbon can be used to help the filter remove a variety of impurities, from unpleasant odors to gases that can irritate the lungs.
The cabin air filter is a relatively new feature in the automotive field and is often overlooked in regular vehicle maintenance. This is unfortunate, because cabin air filters are as important to passengers as engine air filters are to vehicles. Cabin filters handle the air that enters the passenger compartment through the vehicle's heating and air conditioning system, enhancing passenger comfort by trapping particles, pollen and other irritants.
For people with allergies and other sensitivities to airborne substances, cabin filters represent a significant advance. Changing cabin air filters at the recommended intervals, or earlier in areas where soot or pollen is prevalent, allows passengers to breathe cleaner, fresher air - making any journey more enjoyable.
XueYuan replacement cabin air filters are designed to optimize airflow and filtration and fully meet all OE requirements. Up to five layers of filter media are used to capture contaminants as small as 0.001 microns. Just like engine air filters, some discount suppliers are known to cut corners on quality to save a few cents. Price reductions can become uneconomical. What consumers really want is value, and Xueyuan's cabin air filters provide that value.
## Types of filters available.
Charcoal - In addition to filtering airborne contaminants, charcoal is used to eliminate odors such as exhaust fumes entering the passenger compartment and to filter odors such as cigarette smoke from the vehicle. Electrostatic - The filter media is electrostatically charged to trap ultra-fine particles such as cigarette smoke and diesel carbon. Particulate - Filters out pollen, dust, pollutants and other airborne contaminants.
