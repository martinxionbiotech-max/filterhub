## H1

Sourcing HEPA/ULPA filters and cleanroom equipment: what to verify

## Direct answer

Lock the efficiency class to the cleanroom's ISO 14644 target, verify the seal type against the housing, and confirm whether a factory leakage-test certificate is required. For FFU selection, size the unit to the required airflow and cleanliness class rather than matching dimensions alone.

## Efficiency class under EN 1822

HEPA and ULPA grades split into three bands. H10–H12 cover the EPA range, H13–H14 are high-efficiency HEPA (≥99.95% at MPPS), and U15–U17 are ultra-low-penetration ULPA. The choice is not a matter of "higher is better" — a U15 unit costs more and needs a properly graded pre-filter chain upstream to reach its rated life. Match the class to the cleanroom's target, not to a marketing claim.

## Construction: separator vs mini-pleat

HEPA elements are built either with separator pleat-spacing or as compact mini-pleat units. Mini-pleat packs more media area into the same frame, which suits space-constrained housings. Seal type matters more than pleat style for installation: gel seals fit knife-edge housings, gasket and endless-gasket seals fit flanged housings. Frame material — aluminium, galvanised steel or stainless — follows the environment.

## Sizing and panel dimensions

Standard HEPA panels run from 610×610×70 mm up to 610×610×292 mm, with deeper units carrying more media area. Confirm the exact depth, because a 70 mm and a 292 mm unit are not interchangeable in the same housing.

## Fan filter units (FFU)

An FFU combines a fan with a HEPA/ULPA filter in a galvanised or stainless housing. A 48"×48" double-motor unit delivers 4000–5000 CMH at Class 100; smaller 48"×24" and 24"×24" units deliver proportionally less. Efficiency spans F8/F9 (EN 779) through H10–H14/U15 (EN 1822), with 80 °C / 80% RH operating limits. Specify AC or EC motor and voltage (110/220 V) at inquiry, since these affect integration and energy use.

## What to confirm before ordering

- The required efficiency class (H13/H14 vs U15) against the cleanroom's ISO class
- Seal type (gel vs gasket) and frame material
- Exact panel dimensions and depth
- For FFU: required airflow, cleanliness class, motor type and voltage
- Whether a factory leakage-test certificate is required for HEPA units

## What is still unconfirmed

Per-model pressure drop, dust-holding capacity and media grammage are not uniformly published across source catalogues and are marked `pending` where absent. Indicative pricing and lead times for standard HEPA sizes are available on quotation; treat listed figures as indicative, confirmed on the quote.
