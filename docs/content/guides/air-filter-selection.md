## H1

Selecting engine intake air filters: panel, round and heavy-duty

## Direct answer

Start from the OE number or the equipment model, confirm the filter form factor against the housing, then verify media construction for the dust environment. The catalogue's cross-reference is a discovery aid, not a verified fitment.

## Why form factor matters first

Engine intake housings dictate the filter shape. Panel elements fit compact passenger and commercial vehicles; round elements fit agricultural and off-highway equipment; heavy-duty cylindrical cartridges serve trucks and construction machinery. Picking the right form factor eliminates most wrong orders before efficiency is even considered. PowerCore construction adds a compact pleated-media option for housings with limited space.

## Efficiency and dust capacity

Engine intake filtration is graded under ISO 5011, which measures multi-pass efficiency and dust-holding capacity together. A filter that traps particles efficiently but loads up quickly costs more in downtime than one with a balanced efficiency-to-capacity curve. In heavy-dust environments, greater media area extends service intervals more than a marginal efficiency gain.

## Reading the OE cross-reference

Part numbers such as `42N-62-15470` (Komatsu loader), `P778989` (Mitsubishi compressor) and `1542743`/`8-475386` (Volvo) are catalogued as cross-reference entries. A cross-reference links a filter to an OE number; it does not by itself confirm the dimensional match. Request the spec sheet and check against the OE drawing before committing a bulk order.

## What to confirm before ordering

- The exact OE number, or the equipment make, model and year
- The filter form factor (panel / round / heavy-duty / PowerCore)
- The dust environment, to size media area correctly
- Dimensions, pressure drop and dust-holding capacity — published per model only where the source catalogue provides them; otherwise request them

## What is still unconfirmed

Per-model dimensions, pressure drop and dust-holding capacity are not uniformly published across source catalogues. Where a field is absent it is marked `pending` in the data subsite rather than filled with an estimate.
