## H1

Air, oil, fuel, hydraulic and cleanroom filtration — one Qinghe Yinghui-based manufacturing partner

## Positioning statement

Qinghe Yinghui Filter consolidates a full-span filter catalogue under a single sourcing relationship. Engine intake air, cabin, oil, fuel, hydraulic and transmission filters sit alongside HVAC, HEPA/ULPA, activated-carbon and cleanroom-grade equipment, so a buyer carrying several categories no longer has to split orders across multiple suppliers.

## Five questions (B2B trust paradigm)

**Who are you?**
A filter manufacturer and exporter positioned in the Qinghe filter industry cluster of Hebei province. Entity and registration details are disclosed on the company page and remain provisional until business-licence verification.

**What do you make?**
Thirteen product categories spanning engine filtration, HVAC/cleanroom air filtration and liquid/metal cartridges. The full taxonomy and efficiency-grade coverage are listed in the product centre and mirrored in the data subsite.

**Why choose you?**
A consolidated catalogue reduces supplier count. Each category is mapped to an efficiency-grade framework (EN 1822, ISO 16890, EN 779, ASHRAE 52.2) so specifications are comparable across the range. Custom sizing and OEM/private-label are supported.

**How do you guarantee quality?**
Specifications carry a per-field data-status label (verified / catalog-confirmed / reference / pending) so a buyer can see exactly what is sourced and what still needs confirmation. The data subsite holds the machine-readable record behind every claim.

**How do we start?**
Send an inquiry with the SKU or OE number, or a drawing and target quantity. MOQ and lead time are quoted per line; indicative pricing is confirmed on quotation.
