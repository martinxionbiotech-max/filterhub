## Category entry (13 categories)

- **Air filters** — panel, round and heavy-duty engine intake, PowerCore, air-compressor and gas-turbine stages
- **Cabin air filters** — passenger and commercial vehicle cabin filtration
- **Oil filters** — engine and equipment oil filtration
- **Fuel filters** — diesel and petrol fuel filtration, incl. urea
- **Hydraulic filters** — heavy-equipment and industrial hydraulic circuits
- **Transmission filters** — automatic transmission filtration
- **HVAC filters** — pre (G1–G4), medium (M5–F9) and V-bank stages
- **HEPA / ULPA filters** — H10–H14 and U15–U17 for cleanroom and critical environments
- **Activated carbon filters** — odour and chemical-vapour removal
- **Pocket / bag filters** — high dust-holding AHU stages
- **Filter media** — needle-punched, synthetic fibre, micro-glass and activated carbon
- **Liquid filter cartridges** — membrane (PP/PVDF/PTFE) and metal (SS304/SS316L/Ti)
- **Cleanroom equipment** — FFU, laminar flow, biosafety cabinets, air showers, pass boxes

## Product database

Every SKU has a matching machine-readable record on the data subsite, with OE cross-reference,
technical parameters, material, filter-media, application and MOQ fields — each carrying a
data-status label. The dataset is published as a single products.json file.

## Five questions (B2B trust paradigm)

**Who are you?**
A product information and database hub operated by Qinghe County Huiying Automotive Parts Co., Ltd.
— a filter manufacturer based in Qinghe County, Hebei, China. Registration details are disclosed on
the company page.

**What do you make?**
Thirteen product categories spanning engine filtration, HVAC/cleanroom air filtration and
liquid/metal cartridges. The full taxonomy and efficiency-grade coverage are listed in the product
centre and mirrored in the data subsite.

**Why choose you?**
A consolidated catalogue reduces supplier count. Each category is mapped to an efficiency-grade
framework (EN 1822, ISO 16890, EN 779, ASHRAE 52.2) so specifications are comparable across the
range. Custom sizing and OEM/private-label are supported.

**How do you guarantee quality?**
Specifications carry a per-field data-status label (verified / catalog-confirmed / reference /
pending) so a buyer can see exactly what is sourced and what still needs confirmation. The data
subsite holds the machine-readable record behind every claim.

**How do we start?**
Send an inquiry with the SKU or OE number, or a drawing and target quantity. MOQ and lead time are
quoted per line; indicative pricing is confirmed on quotation.

## Strength section

- **Consolidated range** — one relationship across engine, HVAC, cleanroom and liquid filtration.
- **Grade-standardised** — every air-filtration category is anchored to a recognised efficiency class.
- **Traceable data** — each specification field carries a source and a data-status tag; unconfirmed fields stay marked pending, not guessed.
- **Custom and OEM** — standard and custom sizes, with OEM/ODM support.

## Compliance / honesty strip

Specifications on this site are compiled from multiple manufacturer catalogues and standards
references. Fields not independently verified are labelled `pending`. No certification, customer
reference or factory figure is presented without a traceable source.
