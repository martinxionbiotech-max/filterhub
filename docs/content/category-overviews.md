## 1. Air Filters (Engine Intake)

### H1

Engine intake air filters — panel, round and heavy-duty

### Direct answer

An engine air filter removes airborne dust before it reaches the combustion chamber. The catalogue covers panel, round and heavy-duty cartridge forms, plus PowerCore, air-compressor and gas-turbine intake stages.

### Technical depth

Intake air filtration is graded under ISO 5011 for multi-pass efficiency and dust capacity; HVAC-side grades under ISO 16890 apply to the ventilation categories rather than engine intake. Form factor follows the housing: panel elements suit compact passenger and commercial vehicles, round elements suit agricultural and off-highway equipment, and heavy-duty cylindrical elements serve trucks and construction machinery. PowerCore construction packs pleated media into a compact footprint for space-constrained housings.

OE cross-reference is the practical entry point for this category. Part numbers such as `42N-62-15470` (Komatsu loader), `P778989` (Mitsubishi compressor) and `1542743`/`8-475386` (Volvo) are catalogued as cross-reference entries; the exact dimensional match still requires confirmation against the OE drawing because the source catalogue does not publish per-model dimensions.

### Selection guidance

- Confirm the OE number first; treat the catalogue's cross-reference as a starting point, not a verified fitment.
- Verify media type and pleat construction for the dust environment (heavy dust favours greater media area).
- Dimensions, pressure drop and dust-holding capacity are published per model only where the source catalogue provides them; otherwise request the spec sheet.

## 2. Cabin Air Filters

### H1

Cabin air filters for passenger and commercial vehicles

### Direct answer

A cabin filter cleans air entering the vehicle interior, protecting occupants from dust and particulates.

### Technical depth

Cabin filtration sits between engine intake (coarse particle removal) and HVAC-grade media. Particle-holding and odour-removal variants map to the same ISO 16890 / activated-carbon logic as building HVAC, but in a compact automotive cartridge form. The source catalogue confirms the category and typical cross-reference part numbers; per-model efficiency and dimensions are not published and remain `pending`.

### Selection guidance

- Match by OE number; confirm the specific vehicle year and trim before ordering.
- Specify whether particle-only or carbon (odour) media is required.
- Request the spec sheet for dimensions and efficiency class.

## 3. Oil Filters

### H1

Engine and equipment oil filters

### Direct answer

An oil filter removes contaminants from lubricating oil to protect the engine or hydraulic-equipment oil circuit.

### Technical depth

Oil filtration for engines and equipment is catalogued together with hydraulic filtration. The category is OE-cross-referenced; the source catalogue publishes part numbers but not per-model media micron rating, bypass-valve setting or canister dimensions — those fields stay `pending`.

### Selection guidance

- Match by OE number; confirm thread size and canister dimensions on the spec sheet.
- Specify operating oil type and change interval for correct media selection.
- Request bypass-valve and micron-rating data where critical.

## 4. Fuel Filters

### H1

Diesel and petrol fuel filters, incl. urea

### Direct answer

A fuel filter removes water and particulate from fuel before the injection system.

### Technical depth

The catalogue lists diesel fuel filters for heavy-duty trucks and commercial vehicles, with urea filtration listed alongside. OE entries include `VG1540080311`, `8159975`, `2169185` and `23401-1441` for heavy-duty diesel applications. Water-separation capability and micron rating are not published per model and remain `pending`.

- Request micron rating and water-separation efficiency.

## 5. Hydraulic Filters

### H1

Hydraulic circuit filters for heavy equipment and industrial systems

### Direct answer

A hydraulic filter protects pumps, valves and actuators by removing particles from the hydraulic oil circuit.

### Technical depth

Hydraulic filtration is catalogued with oil filtration. Selection depends on system pressure, flow rate and the cleanliness target of the circuit. Per-model pressure rating, micron rating and flow capacity are not published by the source catalogue and remain `pending`.

### Selection guidance

- Provide system flow rate and operating pressure when inquiring.
- Specify the cleanliness class target (ISO 4406) of the circuit.
- Request pressure-drop and beta-ratio data for critical circuits.

## 7. HVAC Filters

### H1

HVAC air filters — pre, medium and V-bank stages

### Direct answer

HVAC filters protect air-handling equipment and occupants by staged particle removal, from coarse pre-filtration through fine filtration.

### Technical depth

HVAC filtration follows a grade ladder. Pre-filters (G1–G4) capture coarse dust and protect downstream stages; medium filters (M5–F9) remove finer particulate; V-bank rigid filters deliver high airflow at low pressure drop for the main AHU stage. Grades are expressed under EN 779 and ISO 16890 (ePM1/ePM2.5/ePM10/coarse). Frame options include cardboard, metal and galvanised steel, with synthetic-fibre or micro-glass media.

### Selection guidance

- Match the filter grade to the downstream protection target (a HEPA stage needs a properly graded pre-filter upstream).
- Confirm frame size and construction (cardboard for disposable, metal for washable or higher strength).
- Request pressure-drop and dust-holding data for energy-consumption comparisons.

## 8. HEPA / ULPA Filters

### H1

HEPA and ULPA filters — H10 through U17

### Direct answer

HEPA/ULPA filters remove ≥99.95% (H13) and up to 99.9995% (U15+) of particles at MPPS for cleanrooms and critical environments.

### Technical depth

Efficiency is graded under EN 1822: H10–H12 cover the EPA range, H13–H14 are high-efficiency HEPA, and U15–U17 are ultra-low-penetration ULPA. Construction is either separator (with pleat-spacing media) or mini-pleat (compact, no separator). Sealing options include gel and endless-gasket types; frames are aluminium, galvanised steel or stainless. Typical panel sizes run 610×610×70 mm through 610×610×292 mm, with deeper units carrying more media area. Mini-pleat production uses micro-glass media with pleat depths of 22–100 mm.

### Selection guidance

- Confirm the required efficiency class (H13/H14 vs U15) against the cleanroom's ISO 14644 class target.
- Verify the seal type matches the housing (gel for knife-edge housings, gasket for flanged).
- Confirm dimensions and whether a factory leakage test certificate is required.

## 9. Activated Carbon Filters

### H1

Activated carbon filters for odour and chemical-vapour removal

### Direct answer

Activated carbon filters adsorb gaseous odours and chemical vapours that particulate filters cannot capture.

### Technical depth

Carbon media appears across several forms — panel, pleated, pocket and V-bank — and is applied to odour control in commercial buildings and chemical-vapour capture in industrial settings. Carbon filters complement rather than replace particulate stages. Media weight and adsorption capacity per model are not published and remain `pending`.

### Selection guidance

- Specify the target contaminant (odour, VOC, or a specific chemical vapour) — carbon grade depends on it.
- Confirm the airflow and contact time required for the target removal efficiency.
- Request carbon weight and expected service life.

## 10. Pocket / Bag Filters

### H1

Pocket and bag filters for high dust-holding AHU stages

### Direct answer

Pocket filters provide high dust-holding capacity with a large media surface area in a compact frame for AHU main filtration.

### Technical depth

Pocket filters use extended media pockets to maximise surface area and dust capacity. Grades run M5 through F9 (EN 779 / ISO 16890). Media is typically synthetic fibre for strength and long service life. Frame and pocket-count options vary by airflow.

### Selection guidance

- Match grade to the AHU's stage (medium-grade pockets are a common main-stage choice).
- Confirm face dimensions and number of pockets against the AHU frame.
- Request initial pressure-drop and dust-holding capacity for life-cycle cost.

## 11. Filter Media

### H1

Filter media — needle-punched, synthetic fibre, micro-glass and activated carbon

### Direct answer

Filter media is the raw filtration material supplied as rolls or made-to-order media for filter manufacturers and integrators.

### Technical depth

Media types span needle-punched non-woven (for pre-filtration and ceiling rolls), synthetic fibre (for HVAC panels and pockets), micro-glass fibre (for HEPA/ULPA) and activated carbon (for odour control). Media is supplied at low MOQ (from 20 rolls) with prototyping-to-production capability. Grammage, thickness and efficiency-curve parameters are not published and remain `pending`.

### Selection guidance

- Specify the target efficiency grade and the application (pre-filter vs HEPA vs odour).
- Confirm roll width, grammage and any fire-rating requirement (e.g. UL 900).
- Request an efficiency curve for the target particle size.

## 12. Liquid Filter Cartridges

### H1

Liquid filter cartridges — membrane and metal

### Direct answer

Liquid filter cartridges remove particles or separate phases from process liquids, using membrane (PP/PVDF/PTFE) or metal (SS304/SS316L/Ti) media.

### Technical depth

Membrane cartridges use PP, PVDF or PTFE membranes with PP/PET/PTFE/PVDF cores and 215/222/226/DOE end-caps, in 5–70-inch lengths, with O-ring materials from silicon to EPDM, Viton and FEP. Metal cartridges use SS304, SS316L or titanium in sintered or woven-mesh form, with filtration ratings from 1 to 100 μm and diameters of 20/38/60 mm or custom.

### Selection guidance

- Specify the fluid, temperature and chemical compatibility to choose the right membrane or metal grade.
- Confirm the micron rating and flow rate.
- For metal cartridges, specify sintered vs woven-mesh construction and end-fitting type.

## 13. Cleanroom Equipment

### H1

Cleanroom equipment — FFU, laminar flow, biosafety cabinets, air showers and pass boxes

### Direct answer

Cleanroom equipment maintains the controlled airflow and contamination control required for ISO-classified cleanrooms.

### Technical depth

Fan filter units (FFU) combine a fan with a HEPA/ULPA filter in a galvanised or stainless housing; a 48"×48" double-motor unit delivers 4000–5000 CMH at Class 100. Efficiency runs F8/F9 (EN 779) through H10–H14/U15 (EN 1822), with 80 °C / 80% RH operating limits. The equipment range extends to laminar-flow cabinets, biosafety cabinets, air showers and pass boxes. Standard and custom sizes are offered, with standard HEPA sizes partially stocked for fast delivery.

### Selection guidance

- Confirm the cleanroom's ISO class and required FFU airflow/coverage.
- Specify AC or EC motor and voltage (110/220 V).
- Confirm dimensions and whether standard or custom sizing is needed.
